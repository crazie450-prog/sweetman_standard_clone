Chapter Nine
Collard Greens and Cornbread…revised 11/2..tested 11/1
My mother made Collard Greens and Cornbread, three times a year. 1st would always be for Thanksgiving. 2nd is Christmas and lastly New Years Day. Collard Greens take a long time to cook, when done properly. In most cases, you are looking at between 4-6 hours of cook time. The versatile vegetable can be used in a number of dishes. Salads, Pesto’s, Dressings/Vinaigrettes and more. I will be taking you on a journey taking this African ingredient and using it across several cultural barriers, to show you that it doesn’t have to have the flavors of the south.

Slow Cooked Classic Collard Greens
Cooked in, “Mother’s Milk Broth” and served with Skillet Cornbread
(Makes 6 servings)

Step 1: Mother’s Milk
***(start this at 10pm the day prior)

2.5 lbs Smoked Ham Hocks
3 quarts Water

Place smoked ham hocks in a large stock pot.
Add water and bring to a boil.
Reduce to heat on low heat and cover with a lid.
Set timer on your phone for 10am.
Cook overnight on low heat.
Next morning strain off the broth through a fine meshed strainer.
Save the stock (aka Mother’s Milk)
Save the meat and the fat from the smoked ham hocks.
Remove the bone.
Chop up the meat and fat.
Reserve the meat and fat to the side for the greens.

Step 2: Collard Greens

4 oz Salt Pork, small diced/ground
2lbs cooked and chopped Smoked Ham Hock Meat
2 quarts “Mother’s Milk”, Smoked Ham Hock Stock
2 cups small diced Spanish Onions
½ cup Fried Shallots, see reference
¼ cup Fried Garlic, see reference
2 cups Apple Cider Vinegar
½ cup Brown Sugar
1 Tbsp Crushed Red Pepper Flakes
2 Cinnamon Sticks
4 (1 Gallon) Ziplock Bags of Chopped Collards
2 Tablespoon Kosher Salt

Place salt pork in microwavable container and cook for 3 minutes in the microwave.
Stir and cook for another 3 minutes.
Place the rendered fat and cracklings from the microwave into a large pot.
Add the cooked smoked ham hock meat, mothers milk, apple cider vinegar, sugar, cinnamon sticks and crushed red pepper flakes.
Bring to a boil.
Add the collard greens and bring to a boil.
Stir thoroughly.
Cover with a lid and turn heat on low.
Set timer and cook on low heat for a 3 hours.
Now add the fried shallots, garlic and kosher salt.
Cook for another hour.
Remove and reserve warm until ready to serve.

Step 3: Skillet Cornbread

8 oz. Salt Pork, ground/small diced
2 cups Self Rising Flour
2 cups Cornmeal
4 Eggs, large
1-1/2 cups Buttermilk
½ cup Water
½ cup Melted Butter
2 cups canned Creamed Corn
2 ears of Corn, shucked and kernels removed from cobb
1 Jalapeno Pepper, chopped
¼ cup Granulated Sugar
1 Tablespoon Kosher Salt

Preheat oven to 400 F.
Place salt pork in a microwavable container and place into a microwave for 3 minutes.
Stir and repeat for another 3 minutes.
Place all of the rendered fat into a large cast iron skillet (12”).
Now place flour, cornmeal, sugar, salt, shucked corn, creamed corn, jalapeno and salt pork crackling into a bowl and mix thoroughly. 
Add buttermilk, water, eggs and melted butter.
Mix until batter is slightly smooth.
Place the skillet in the oven and set timer for 5 minutes.
Remove the skillet from the over and pour the batter into the skillet.
Place skillet into the oven and set timer for 15 minutes.
Rotate cornbread and set timer for another 9 minutes.
Remove cornbread from oven and rub the top of the cornbread with a stick of salted butter.
Allow to cool to room temperature.

Step 4: The Build

Greens
Cornbread

Place 2 cups of Greens into a serving bowl.
Serve with a wedge of Cornbread.

Thai Style Collard Green Salad
Raw Collard Greens, Dried Shrimp, Fish Sauce, Palm Sugar, Bird Chilis, Peanuts, Tomatoes & Peppers, served with a Thai Coconut Cornbread Crepe
(Makes 6 Servings)

Step 1: Thai Coconut Cornbread Crepe (Makes 12-13 one oz Crepes)

½ cup Self-Rising Flour
½ cup Cornmeal
¼ cup Sugar
1 tablespoon Ground Ginger
1 tsp Kosher Salt
2 Eggs
1 cup Coconut Milk
¼ cup Coconut Oil

Preheat a pancake griddle or tephlon pan on medium heat.
Place self rising flour, cornmeal, sugar, ginger and salt into a bowl.
Add eggs, coconut milk and coconut oil.
Mix thoroughly.
Place a ¼ cup of batter on to the hot griddle.
Cook for 1 minute or until the top has lots of bubbles on the surface.
Flip over and cook for another 30 seconds.
Remove and repeat until all crepes are made.
Stack the crepes on top of one another and cover with a towel and reserve warm until ready to serve.

Step 2: Thai Style Collard Green Salad

¼ cup dried Shrimp
½ cup Palm Sugar
6 Garlic Cloves
2 Bird Chilis, stemmed
½ cup Toasted Peanuts
¼ cup Fish Sauce or Golden Mountain Boy Seasoning Sauce
¼ cup Fresh Lime Juice
1 Tablespoon Kosher Salt
1 cup Red Grape Tomatoes
½ cup medium diced Red Bell Peppers
½ cup Thai Basil Leaves
6 cups julienne Collard Greens

You will need 1 large wooden Pestle Mortar.
Place dried shrimp, palm sugar, garlic cloves and bird chilis inside and proceed to pound.
Pound until ingredients are crushed thoroughly.
Now add grape tomatoes, peppers and salt.
Pound together gently.
Now add fish and lime juice.
Mash together thoroughly.
Now add the collard greens and pound/mash together until the salad comes together.
Now add the peanuts and thai basil.
Combine together gently.
Reserve until ready to serve.

Step 3: The Build

Thai Collard Green Salad
Cornbread Crepes

Place Salad in a large serving Bowl.
Place crepes on a large platter.

Collard Green Minestrone
Spicy Italian Sausage Tomato Broth with Black Eyed Peas, Vegetables, Collard Greens + Collard Green Pesto, served with a side Cornbread Panzanella Salad
(Makes 6 Servings)

Step 1: Collard Green Pesto

1 cup Olive Oil, pomace
5 Garlic Cloves
¼ cup Fresh Lemon Juice
¼ cup Pignoli Nuts
¼ cup Parmesan Cheese
½ cup Sage Leaves
½ cup Italian Parsley Leaves
2 cups stemmed and chopped Collard Greens
¼ teaspoon Kosher Salt

Place olive oil, garlic, lemon juice, salt and parmesan into a blender and puree until smooth.
Add the sage, Italian parsley and collard greens.
Puree until smooth.
Remove and reserve.

Step 2: Minestrone Soup

1 lbs Spicy Italian Sausage
½ cup Olive Oil, pomace
¼ cup chopped Garlic
1 quart V8 Juice
1 quart Chicken Stock
1 cup diced Canned Tomatoes
1 cup cooked Black Eyed Peas
1 cup medium diced Red Peppers
1 cup medium diced Carrots
1 cup medium diced Celery
1 cup medium diced Spanish Onions
3 cups medium diced Collard Greens
½ cup Datalini Pasta, cooked
¼ cup Italian Parsley, chopped

Place Italian sausage into a large pot with olive oil.
Turn on medium heat.
Brown the sausage.
Add the garlic, onions, carrots, celery, peppers and sweat for 2 minutes.
Add the V8 Juice and Chicken Stock.
Bring to a boil and reduce to a simmer.
Add collard greens and mix well.
Cover the pot and cook for 30 minutes.
Now add the black eyed peas, and pasta. Italian parsley.
Bring to a simmer.
Reserve warm.

Step 3: Panzanella Salad

3 cups Large Diced Cornbread 
1 cup Olive Oil
¼ cup chopped Garlic
½ cup small diced Red Peppers
½ cup small diced Red Onions
½ cup small diced Fennel
1 cup Basil Leaves
½ cup White Balsamic Vinegar
¼ cup Honey
1 cup Chiffonade Collard Greens
½ cup Picked Basil Leaves
1 cup Grated Parmesan Cheese

Preheat oven to 400 F.
Place cornbread on a sheet pan and place into oven.
Toast cornbread for 10 minutes.
Rotate cornbread and cook for another 5 minutes.
Place olive oil in a large skillet on medium heat.
Add the peppers, onions and fennel.
Get a little caramelization on the vegetables.
Turn heat on low heat now.
Add the cornbread and toss together gently.
Combine balsamic vinegar and honey together in a separate bowl.
Dress the cornbread salad with dressing.
Fold in Collard Greens and Basil.
Place into a large Bowl.
Garnish with Parmesan Cheese.

Step 4: The Build
(Family Style)

Large Pot of Soup
Side Bowl of Salad
Side Bowl of Pesto
Side Bowl of Parmesan Cheese

Kimchi Collard Green Soup
Grilled Beef Short Ribs simmered with Kimchi Collard Green Stems + Young Collard Greens, Cornbread Dumplings, Peppers and Garlic
(Makes 6 Servings)

Step 1: Kimchi Collard Green Stems
(Prepare 24 hours – 1 week before)

4 cups Collard Green Stems, cut into 1” pieces
½ cup Garlic Cloves, halved
¾ cup Kimchi Base, see page reference

Combine all ingredients into a large bowl and mixing bowl and combine thoroughly.
Place ingredients into a 1-gallon sized Ziplock bag and seal.
Place into the refrigerator for a minimum of 24 hours up to 1 week.

Step 2: Cornbread Dumplings (Potato Gnocchi)

2 Idaho Potatoes
1 cup Self-Rising Flour
1 cup Corn Meal
1 Tablespoon Ground Ginger
1 tsp Kosher Salt
2 Eggs
¼ cup Melted Butter

For Dusting
½ cup Self-Rising Flour
½ cup Cornmeal Flour

Place potatoes into a pot and cover with cold water.
Place the pot onto the stove and bring to a boil and reduce to a simmer.
Cook the potatoes until they are fork tender. ***Est: 25-30 minutes.
Drain the water from the potatoes and carefully peel away the skin and discard.
Sift together the flour, cornmeal, salt and ground ginger.
Place on a counter surface.
Rice the potatoes over the flour mixture.
Cut in the hot potatoes into the flour mixture with a bench scraper.
Add the melted butter and eggs.
Gently bring the dough together until it forms a smooth dough.
Cover with a towel and allow to rest for 5 minutes.
Cut the dough into 4 equal pieces.
Lightly dust the counter surface with the flour.
Roll one piece of dough into about 12-14” long roll.
Line a sheet pan with parchment paper and lightly dust with semolina flour.
Cut the dumplings into 1” pieces. 
Lightly dust with semolina flour.
Gently scoop up and place on to sheet pan.
Repeat until all dumplings are made.
Place dumplings into the refrigerator and allow to cool down. ***Est: 15 minutes.
Place a large pot of salted water on the stove and bring to a boil.
Scoop up the dumplings with a bench scrapper and place into the boiling water, while stirring gently. ***Note: only add a small number of dumplings at a time.
Set up a bowl of ice water to cool down the dumplings.
Once the dumplings begin to float, allow to cook for an additional 2 minutes.
Remove and place into the ice bath.
Remove from the ice bath and toss with vegetable oil to prevent sticking.
Reserve.

Step 3: Kimchi Soup

2 lbs. Boneless Beef Short Ribs, cut into 1 oz. pieces
½ cup Sesame Oil
1 Tablespoon Kosher Salt
1 Tablespoon Fresh Ground Black Pepper
½ cup Lite Soy Sauce
¼ cup Sesame Oil
2 quarts Chicken Stock
3 cups Kimchi Collard Green Stems + Garlic
2 cups Chiffonade Young Collard Greens
½ cup quartered Baby Turnips
½ cup halved Cherry Tomatoes, ½ cup medium diced Carrots 
½ cup medium diced Red Peppers
½ cup Kimchi Base
36 pieces Cornbread Dumplings

Preheat Charcoal Grill.
Combine in a large ziplock back, short ribs, sesame oil, kosher salt and soy sauce.
Marinate a minimum of 30 minutes.
Grill the short ribs for 3 minutes on each side.
Place chicken stock, kimchi collard green stems + garlic and kimchi base on the stove and bring to a boil.
Reduce to a simmer. 
Cook for 10 minutes.
Now add the grilled beef short ribs.
Cook for 45 minutes.
Now add the young collard greens, carrots, turnips, peppers and dumplings.
Cook for another 5 minutes.
Garnish with Cherry Tomatoes.
Remove and serve.

Step 4: The Build
(Family Style)

Large Pot of Kimchi Soup
Picked Cilantro
Baby Radish with Tops
Side Bowl of Fried Garlic
Side Bowl of Steamed Rice

Jerk Style Pork Chop
Coconut Curry Collard Greens, Pineapple Cornbread Gremolata
(Makes 6 Servings)

Step 1: Jerk Pork Chop
(Overnight)

6 six oz pieces of Pork Chop
1 cup Vegetable Oil
1 each Habanero Pepper, stemmed
½ cup Chef Kenny’s Jerk Spice
½ cup large diced Spanish Onion
½ cup Green Bell Pepper
¼ cup Italian Parsley
¼ cup Marjoram or Oregano
5 Garlic Cloves
½ cup Spiced Rum

Place oil, habanero pepper, jerk spice, onion, bell pepper, parsley, marjoram or oregano and spice rum.
Puree until smooth.
Place pork chops and marinade in a Ziplock bag and marinate overnight.
Preheat Charcoal Grill.
Remove the pork chops from the refrigerator and allow to get to room temperature.
Grill pork chops 4-5 minutes on each side or until an internal temperature of 145-155 F.
Allow to rest 10 minutes before serving.

Step 2: Coconut Curry Collard Greens
4 (one Gallon) Ziplock Bags of Collard Greens, stemmed and cut into large, diced pieces
½ cup Vegetable Oil
1 cup Chef Kenny’s Curry Starter (Ginger, Onion, Garlic and Ghee Puree)..see page reference
½ cup Madras Yellow Curry Powder
4 cups Coconut Milk
1 cup small diced Spanish Onions
1 each chopped Habanero Pepper
¼ cup Granulated White Sugar
¼ cup Lite Soy Sauce
¼ cup Fresh Lime Juice

Place collard greens in a large pot of water.
Bring to a boil.
Reduce to a simmer and cook for 1 hour.
Drain the greens and reserve.
In a large pot add the vegetable oil, Chef Kenny’s Curry Starter, curry powder, Spanish onions and habanero pepper.
Cook on medium heat for 2-3 minutes, stirring often.
Add the coconut milk, sugar, soy and lime juice and bring to a simmer.
Add the cooked collard greens and bring back up to a simmer.
Cook on low to medium heat for 30 minutes.
Reserve warm.

Step 3: Pineapple Cornbread Gremolata

1 cup Cooked and Crumbled Cornbread
½ cup crushed small, diced Pineapple
¼ cup minced Spanish Onion
2 Tablespoons minced Garlic
1 cup chopped Italian Parsley
½ cup chopped Cilantro
Juice from 1 Lime
Kosher Salt to taste

Preheat oven to 350 F.
Place cornbread crumbles on to a small baking sheet and place into the oven.
Bake for 10 minutes.
Stir the cornbread mixture.
Bake for another 5 minutes. 
Remove and cool down.
Place cornbread crumbles into a bowl and add the rest of the ingredients.
Mix gently.
Reserve.

Step 4: The Build

Grilled Jerk Pork Chop
Curry Collard Greens
Pineapple Gremolata 
Charred Lime Wedge

Place 1 cup of Curried Collards in the center of a plate.
Place Pork Chop on top of greens.
Garnish the Top of the Pork Chop with the Pineapple Gremolata.
Place a charred Lime on the side of the dish.
