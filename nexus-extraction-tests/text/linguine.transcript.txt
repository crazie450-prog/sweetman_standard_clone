In a saute pan drizzle some cooking oil. In high-medium heat combine shallots and garlic without burning or browning. Add half of the White Wine. Add clams. Cook for 5-8 minutes until clams begin to open. Add remaining white wine. Incorporate the pasta. Ocasionaly toss pasta to get an evenly cooked pasta. When pasta is al dente add butter, tomatoes, and parsley. If its to dry add pasta water so it can have a good amount of sauce. Add lemon juice. Toss and serve.
