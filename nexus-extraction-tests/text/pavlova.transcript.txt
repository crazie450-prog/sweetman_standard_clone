Mango Pavlova
Pavlova (Yield 12 units)
-12 large egg whites (room temperature) ($3.17)
-1 tsp cream of tartar ($0.10)
-550 grams sugar ($0.89)
-2 Tbls corn starch ($0.15)
-2 tsp vanilla ($0.97)
Preheat oven to 250°F. In mixing bowl add egg whites and cream of tartar. Whisk until soft peaks appear. Slowly incorporate sugar and whisk until stiff peaks form. Add cornstarch and vanilla and fold with rubber spatula until just incorporated. In a half sheet tray with parchment paper plop down mix to individual servings and shape with spoon. Bake for 1.5 hours and let cool.
Mango Filling
-4 cup cubed mangoes ($2.60)
-3 tsp passion fruit puree ($0.90)
-2 tsp lime juice ($0.15)
In a mixing bowl add all ingredients and mix well. Place mix in middle of pavlova.
