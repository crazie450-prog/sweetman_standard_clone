Caviar, Smoked Salmon, and Gravlax with Buckwheat Blini and Sour Cream

½ tsp active dry yeast
½ c warm milk 110°F
1 large egg
1 tsp sugar
½ tsp kosher salt
2/3 c AP flour
1 c cold whole milk
¼ c clarified butter

4 slices sliced smoked sturgeon
4 slices sliced smoked salmon
4 slices sliced home-cured salmon gravlax

Sour Cream 
2 oz salmon caviar
½ bunch chives, thinly sliced

Blini Batter: sprinkle yeast over warm milk and stir to dissolve. Separate the egg and put the yolk in a large bowl. Cover and refrigerate the white. Add sugar, salt, 1/3 c AP flour, and 1/3 c buckwheat flour to the yolk and whisk . Add yeast mixture and mix until smooth, cover with plastic, put in a warm place until doubled in volume. Add the cold milk and remaining  1/3 c AP flour and 1/3 c buckwheat flour whisk until smooth. Let it rise again for 30 min. To cook blini, beat egg white until stiff peak foam. Gently fold in blini batter. In a nonstick pan add clarified butter and let it get warm and add some of the batter, when the side is light brown flip to cook other side.
