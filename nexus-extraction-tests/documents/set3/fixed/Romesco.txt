Sheet: Sheet1
	wolfgang puck bar and grill
	recipe name:  romesco
	yield: 200 pan
qty.	product
1/3 pan	red bell peppers, membrane removed then julienned
1/3 pan	yellow bell peppers, membrane removed then julienned
1/6 pan	white onion, French cut
1/2 c	garlic
3 T	fennel seed
2 c	almond slivers
2 T	sugar
4 T	white balsamic
to taste	salt
to taste	pepper
3 T	olive oil
	method:
1	sweat onions in oil, on low heat, until translucent
2	turn heat to medium and add almonds and fennel seed, lightly
	toasting them
3	add remaining ingredients and turn heat back to low
4	stir frequently, cooking until peppers are soft
5	cool
