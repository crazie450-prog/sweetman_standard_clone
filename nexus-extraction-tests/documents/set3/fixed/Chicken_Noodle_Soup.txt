Chicken Noodle Soup
2 gallons Chicken Stock
3 cups medium dice onion
2 cups medium dice celery
2 cups medium dice carrot
Sachet – 1 bunch thyme, 6 piece crushed garlic, 3 bay leaves, 10 peppercorns
3 Chickens trussed and stuffed with thyme and parsley stems

Sauté onions and garlic in oil till translucent. Season lightly
Add carrots and celery. Season lightly
Add cold stock, add chickens.
Bring to boil and simmer till chickens are cooked through. (about 2 hours)
Remove chickens cool down. Discard sachet.
Season and cool soup
Pick chicken in bite size chunks and return to cooled soup
Cook low mein noodles in boiling salted water for 12 seconds. Shock in ice water. Drain and toss in olive oil.
Portion soup into vac bags. 8oz
Picked chicken 2.0 oz
Cooked Noodle 1.5 oz
