Sheet: Sheet1
	wolfgang puck bar and grill
	recipe name:  mustard sauce
	yield:  2 quarts
qty.	product
1 c	shallots, rough chopped
3 sprigs	thyme
1 ea	bay leaf
12 ea	white peppercorns
4 c	white wine
1 c	white port
1/2 c	brandy
1 g	chicken stock
1/2 g	heavy cream
2 c	veal demi
4 T	dijon
4 T	wholegrain mustard
1/4 lb	butter
1 t	sugar
to taste	salt
to taste	pepper
	method:
1	in a medium sauce pot place shallots, thyme, bay, peppercorns,
	white wine, port and brandy, reduce by half
2	add cream and reduce by half
4	add demi and chicken stock and reduce to a nape
5	pass through a chinois and return to a pot
6	whisk in mustards and sugar, return to a low heat
7	mount with butter and season
8	hold for service
