Artichoke Puree

8 each		jumbo artichokes
3 T 		butter
To cover 	heavy cream
To taste	salt

Method:
Peel artichoke stem and slice into ¼ inch rings.  Trim top down to heart of the choke, slice ¼ inch thin.  Sauté in butter with salt, covered with parchment, until tender.  Cover with cream and cook out more.  Separate cooked chokes from liquid and place chokes in a high speed blender, adding more liquid if necessary.  Pass through medium tami.

Indian style:
When heating for service add 1/2 t curry powder and 1 t garam masala to 1 cup choke puree, plus cream as needed.
