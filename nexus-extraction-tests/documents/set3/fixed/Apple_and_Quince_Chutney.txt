Apple and Quince Chutney

2 lbs.		quince Paste
1 1/2 lbs.	quince apple, diced (if quince apples are not available,
then use granny smith)
8 oz	      	raisins
1/2 cup		white wine
1/4 cup		vinegar
1/4 cup		granulated sugar
1 each		grated orange zest and juice
salt and pepper
fresh thyme
fresh sage


Sweat diced apples in olive oi.  Deglaze with white wine, then add the sugar, vinegar and orange juice.  Reduce by half.  Add the quince paste, remove from heat and add raisins, orange zest, salt, pepper, thyme and sage.
