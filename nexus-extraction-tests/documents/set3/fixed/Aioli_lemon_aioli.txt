	wolfgang puck bar and grill
	recipe name:  lemon aioli
	yield:  2 quarts
qty.	product
2 cloves	garlic
3 ea	egg yolk
1/4  c	lemon juice
2 c	olive oil blend
4 T	cool water
l ea	zest from one lemon
1 t	dijon
1 T	parsley, chopped
to taste	salt
to taste	pepper
	method:
1	in a robot coupe, place garlic, zest, dijon, and yolk
2	blend, slowly emulsifying in oil
3	as aioli thickens, use the water to thin, keeping a "saucy"
	consistency
4	after all  oil is added, add lemon juice and parsley, mix briefly
5	season and cool
