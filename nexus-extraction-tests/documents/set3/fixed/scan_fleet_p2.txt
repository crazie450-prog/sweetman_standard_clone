Fleetwood's on Front St
Blanch Rosemary Fingerling Potatoes
Yield: 8 Quarts
8qts Fingerling Potatoes
6sprigs Fresh Rosemary
6each Garlic Cloves
1/4cup Extra Virgin Olive Oil
1each Salt (to season)
1. Combine all ingredients, add 1 1/2 times water
2. Bring to a boil, reduce to a simmer until potatoes are just tender
3. Pour into a shallow pan and cool to room temperature in the liquid.
4. Label and Date
