Sheet: Sheet1
	wolfgang puck bar and grill
	recipe name: blue cheese sauce
	yield:
qty.	product
1#	butter
1 c	flour
1 gal	whole milk
3 c	crème fraiche
3 #	Maytag blue cheese
1/4 c	chives, chopped
to taste	salt
to taste	pepper
	method:
1	make a blonde roux with the butter and flour
2	add milk and slowly simmer whisking constantly
3	when thickened, pass through a chinois and return to a clean pot
4	turn to low heat and whisk in crumbled blue cheese
5	add chives
6	season appropriately and cool
