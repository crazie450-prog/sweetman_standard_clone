Parsnip Puree

5 Pounds Parsnips (peeled and sliced thin)
2 Pounds Yukon Potatoes
18 Cups Water
¼ Cup Salt
¼ tsp White Pepper (ground fine)
1 Cup Butter
4 Cup Cream
2 T Thyme, chopped fine
Sugar (optional)

Method:

In a large pot add parsnips, potatoes, water and salt.  
Bring to a simmer and cook until potatoes are tender and cook through.  
Drain and pass though a food mill.  
Stir in butter, pepper and cream.  Pass through fine tamis.  
Add thyme and adjust seasoning and sweetness with sugar if necessary
