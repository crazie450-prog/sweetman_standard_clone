COLD MOUNTAIN MISO
MISO SOUP
Combine 1/2 cup of water and 1 level teaspoon miso (or more to your taste). Mix well. STOVE: In a small pot, bring to boil. DO NOT OVER BOIL. MICROWAVE: Use a microwave safe bowl. Microwave on HIGH 1 minute or until hot. Add desired amount of chopped tofu and / or sea vegetables (wakame). Garnish with chopped green onions and a twist of lemon peel.
MISO SALAD DRESSING
MAKES 1 CUP: 4 1/2 tablespoons Cold Mountain Miso, 4 1/2 tablespoons honey, 3 tablespoons rice vinegar or wine vinegar, 3 tablespoons mustard. Combine all ingredients in a bowl, mix well. Serve with any salads.
Because our miso is all natural (unpasteurized, with no preservatives and high in enzymatic activity), a slight swelling of this container may occur.
