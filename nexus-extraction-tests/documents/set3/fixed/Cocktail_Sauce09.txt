Cocktail Sauce

4 oz Orange Juice
2 oz Lemon Juice
2 oz Lime Juice
3 Cups Ketchup
¼ tsp Maggie Seasoning
¼ Cup Horseradish, Prepared
¼ Cup Worcestershire Sauce
½ Cup Sugar
1 T Salt

In medium bowl mix all ingredients together well. Pass through a chinois.  Store in airtight container in the refrigerator.
