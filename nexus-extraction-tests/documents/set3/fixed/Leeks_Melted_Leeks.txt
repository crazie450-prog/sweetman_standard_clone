Melted Leeks

Ingredients:
2 Leeks 
2 Tbs. Butter
Blanching Water (Water & Salt)
½ c. Chicken Stock
Pinch of Salt

Method:
Peel leeks and discard the outer layer then cut off the root end of the leek, cut leeks into rounds and wash clean. Put leeks into boiling hot blanching water for 4 minutes and into a sauté pan with melted butter cook on low heat and add chicken stock. Cook until tender and add a pinch of salt.
