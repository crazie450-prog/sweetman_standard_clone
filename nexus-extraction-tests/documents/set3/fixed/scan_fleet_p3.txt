Fleetwood's on Front St
Caramelized Maui Onions
Yield: 3 Quarts
20each Maui Onions (sliced thin into rings)
1cup Soy Sauce
2Tbsp Olive Oil (to sauté)
1each Salt and Pepper (to season)
1. In very hot pan, sear and then continue to brown the onions stirring occasionally, scrape the sugars from the bottom of the pan.
2. Season with soy sauce, salt and pepper, do not reduce the soy sauce
3. Pour out onto a parchment paper lined sheet tray
4. Cool to room temperature
5. Label and date
