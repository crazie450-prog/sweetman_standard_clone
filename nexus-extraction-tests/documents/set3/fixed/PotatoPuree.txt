Potato Puree 

Ingredients:
10 # Peeled Yukon Gold Potatoes
2 Cup Butter
2 Cup Crème Fraiche
2 cup Heavy Cream (as needed)
Salt, to taste
Chopped Parsley for garnish


In a Large Pot add potatoes, cover with water and season with salt.
Bring to a boil, cook till just barely tender.
Drain and let steam off.
Rice.
Fold in Butter, And Crème Fraiche, (DO NOT OVER WORK).
Add Cream as Needed.
Pass thru tamis.
Hold Warm.


For Pick up 
Scoop into a side bowl, top with Chopped Parsley
