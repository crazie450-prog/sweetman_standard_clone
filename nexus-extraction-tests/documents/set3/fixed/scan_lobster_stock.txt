Lobster Stock
Yields 34 Quarts
For the Stock
30# cleaned lobster heads
2 qt diced onions
2 qt celery chopped
2 qt chopped leeks rinsed (no dirt or grit)
2 qt carrots chopped
3 qt tomato paste
1 bunch parsley leaves
5 bay leaves
1/2 cu whole black peppercorn
6 qt chicken stock
60 qt water
1. Saute lobster heads in olive oil until browned
2. Crush the shells thoroughly with a wood dow
3. Add tomato paste and brown
4. Add the vegetables
5. Sweat aromatics, water, stock and seasonings
6. Bring to a boil then reduce to a simmer. Simmer for 45 min. Skimming the impurities and tasting until the flavor comes through.
7. Turn off heat and let stock rest for 30 min
8. Blend with immersion blender to break up the shells and strain through chinios
9. Label and Date
