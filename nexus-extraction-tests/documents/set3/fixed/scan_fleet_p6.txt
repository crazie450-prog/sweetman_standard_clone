Fleetwood's on Front St
Sweet Onion Puree
Yield: 2 Quarts
20each Peeled Onions (cut in 1/2)
8sprigs Fresh Thyme
1/4cup Blended Olive Oil
1lb Shallots
1each Salt and Pepper (to season)
1. Add all ingredients to a mixing bowl, mix well
2. Wrap in foil en popiette 5 onions at a time
3. Bake in the convection oven on a sheet pan rack at 350 degrees for 30 minutes or until he onions are soft
4. While hot, strain the oil from the rest of the ingredients, save the oil
5. Puree the shallots with the rest of the ingredients smooth in a vita prep blender, season with salt
6. Cool to 40 degrees
7. Label and date
