Cooking Baby Artichokes
1/2 cs Baby Artichokes
4 qts Cold water
1/2 bu Parsley
1/2 cu Thyme Sprigs
1 Tbsp Black peppercorns
3 ea Bay Leaves
3 cu White Wine
1/2 cu Chopped garlic
2 cu Chopped Carrot
6 cu Chopped White Onion
2 cu Celery
2 cu Chopped Fennel
3/4 cu Extra Virgin Olive Oil
Salt and Pepper to Season
1. Sweat all vegetables and Garlic in Extra Virgin Olive Oil until tender.
2. Add White wine and reduce by 1/2
