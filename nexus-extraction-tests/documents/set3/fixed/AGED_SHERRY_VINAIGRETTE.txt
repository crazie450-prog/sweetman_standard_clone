AGED SHERRY VINAIGRETTE

Yield:  1 1/2 cups



2 oz       pasteurized eggs
2 oz       aged sherry vinegar

8 oz       canola oil

2 tsp      dijon mustard
1 tsp      sugar

to taste   salt and pepper


1.  In a mixing bowl - whip eggs until slightly frothy.

2.  Slowly whisk oil into eggs in a slow steady stream until     emulsified.

3.  Add remaining ingredients and mix well.  Keep refrigerated until     ready to use.
