Béarnaise
Yield: 1 ½qts.
  
Ingredients:

1qt. clarified butter
8ea egg, yolks
½C tarragon reduction
2T salt
1t sugar
1t lemon juice
¼C chopped tarragon
1 T Dijon Mustard

Heat clarified butter in a 2qt. pot. no hotter than 100°F.
In a large mixing bowl add egg yolks, tarragon reduction, salt, sugar, and lemon juice.
Wisk together well
Over a double boiler cook egg mixture until thick ribbons form.
Turn heat off, and slowly wisk clarified butter into egg mixture to form an emulsion. (Use all of the clarified butter) 
Add chopped tarragon
Add Dijon Mustard
Pour into insolated baine marie
