CHICKEN TIKKA MASALA 
 
Ingredients for chicken marinade: 
5 pounds chicken boneless skinless thighs 
1 cup garlic ginger onion lime puree (¼ c. each garlic, ginger, onion, lime juice) 
2 tablespoons ground fenugreek 
1 tablespoon cumin seed 
1 tablespoon ground coriander 
1 teaspoon cayenne pepper 
2 tablespoons paprika 
1/2 cup lite Greek yogurt 
1 tablespoon kosher salt 
 
Method: 
Combine ingredients and marinate for 2 hours.  Roast chicken at 500° F until it reaches an internal temperature of 160°F about 8 minutes in convection oven. 
 
Ingredients for masala: 
1 tablespoon ghee 
1 cup onion slices 
¼ cup ginger sliced 
1 tablespoon sliced garlic 
½ cup garlic, ginger, onion, lime puree 
¼ cup fenugreek seed 
2 tablespoons coriander 
2 tablespoons cumin  
1 tablespoon crushed red pepper flakes 
3 curry leaves or bay leaves 
1 quart crushed tomatoes 
1/2 cup heavy cream 
1 tablespoon kosher salt 
 
Method: 
Heat ghee in pot and sweat onions, ginger, garlic, spices. Add tomatoes and cook for 30 minutes.  
When ready to serve, heat up tomatoes with chicken and finish with cream.  Simmer for 20 minutes with chicken in sauce. 
 
prep time: 30 minutes (plus 2 hrs for chicken to marinate) cook time: 30 minutes  
yeild: 12 (8oz) portions
