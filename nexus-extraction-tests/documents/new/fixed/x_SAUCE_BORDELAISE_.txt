Bordelaise sauce

RED WINE             						1 BOTTLE
PORT                      						2 CUPS
 THYME                   						1 SPRIG
BLACK PEPPERCORNS, TOASTED AND CRACKED 	1 T
SHALLOTS, SLICED              				1#
GARLIC, SMASHED              				2 PICES
BEEF SCRAPS         						1#
SHERRY VINEGAR               				½ CUP
OLIVE OIL               						1T
VEAL DEMI              						1 1/4 CUP
CHICKEN STOCK      					½ GAL
BUTTER                   						6 OZ.
SALT                          					AS NEEDED
PEPPER                       					AS NEEDED

IN A HOT SAUCE POT ADD OLIVE OIL, SHALLOTS, GARLIC AND BEEF SCRAPS, AND PEPPERCORNS OVER MEDIUM TO HIGH HEAT, CARAMELIZE TILL ALL NATURAL SUGARS ARE RELEASED. ADD THYME, SHERRY VINEGAR COOK IT UNTIL IT JUST ABOUT DISAPPEARS THEN ADD RED WINE AND PORT. REDUCE THE WINES UNTIL THEY ARE A SYRUP CONSITENCY. WHEN WINES ARE SYRUP LIKE ADD VEAL DEMI AND CHICKEN STOCK, SIMMER THIS UNTIL IT REDUCES DOWN ABOUT ¾ THE ORIGINAL VOLUME. REMOVE FROM HEAT ADD WHISK IN BUTTERUNTIL IT TOTALLY IS EMULSIFIED IN TO THE SAUCE BASE. ADD SALT AND PEPPER TO TASTE.

TRUFFLE SAUCE

TRUFFLE JUS       						1 OZ.
TRUFFLE PEELINGS  					3 OZ
 
PUREE IN BLENDER UNTIL SMOOTH

SHALLOTS, SLICED         					2 EA
WHITE WINE                     					1 CUP
CHICKEN STOCK              					1 CUP
HEAVY CREAM                					 ½ CUP
SALT                                      				AS NEEDED
PEPPER                                 					AS NEEDED
BUTTER                                 				1 T
TRUFFLE PUREE               					4 OZ 

IN A SAUCE POT ADD WINE AND SHALLOTS REDUCE WINE UNTIL IT GETS TO 2 T. ADD CHICKEN STOCK. REDUCE CHICKEN STOCK BY ¾ THE VOLUME. ADD HEAVY CREAM REDUCE THE CREAM BY HALF. WHISK IN TRUFFLE PUREE. REMOVE FROM HEAT AND WHISK IN BUTTER ADD SALT AND PEPPER TO TASTE.
