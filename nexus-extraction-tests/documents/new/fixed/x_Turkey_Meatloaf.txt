TURKEY MEATLOAF  
 
 
Prep time:30 minutes 
Cook time:25­30 minutes  
Yield: 3 portions 
 
Ingredients: 
1 carrot, peeled, fine dice 
½ onion, fine dice 
1 celery stalk, fine dice  
3 cloves garlic, micro plane  
¼ teaspoon ground black pepper 
½ teaspoon kosher salt 
1 pound turkey thigh ­ ground  
½  egg, beaten 
¼ cup chicken stock 
¼ cup bread crumbs ­ Panko 
1 teaspoon thyme, dried  
1 teaspoon blackening spice  
1 teaspoon parsley, chopped  
 
Chipotle Glaze 
 
Method: 
Sweat vegetables in skillet. Add seasoning and chill. Combine turkey and egg. Add remaining ingredients except for glaze. Form into loaf shape. Place in convection oven at 300* F until it reaches an internal temp of 165* F about 25 minutes. Let rest to room temp. Cover with Chipotle Ketchup Glaze. Broil to caramelize. 
 
Glaze:  
⅓ cup ketchup  
1 teaspoon cumin 
½ teaspoon Worcestershire sauce 
1 teaspoon chipotle paste 
1 teaspoon agave 
¼ teaspoon ground black pepper  
½ teaspoon kosher salt 
 
Method: 
 
Combine all ingredients and bring to the boil and remove from the heat.
