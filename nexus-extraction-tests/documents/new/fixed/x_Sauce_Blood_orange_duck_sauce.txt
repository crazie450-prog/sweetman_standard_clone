	wolfgang puck bar and grill
	recipe name: Blood Orange Duck Sauce
	yield:
qty.	product
1/2 gal	blood orange juice
3/4 can	coconut milk
2 T	shallots, diced
2 T	ginger, diced
2 T	garlic, diced
1 T	5- spice powder
1 stick	cinnamon
2	cloves
pinch	cracked black pepper
1 c	red wine
1 c	port wine
1 c	plum wine (optional)
2 c	veal demi
6 c	chicken stock
	method:
1	combine blood orange juice and coconut milk and reduce until thick
2	sweat together shallot, ginger, and garlic until translucent
3	add 5 spice powder or cinnamon stick,  2 cloves, and pinch of blk. Pepper
4	combine wines and reduce by 3/4 to nappe
5	add stocks
6	combine stock/wine reduction and juice reduction
7	finish with small amount of butter, salt and pepper
8	strain and garnish with cracked black pepper
