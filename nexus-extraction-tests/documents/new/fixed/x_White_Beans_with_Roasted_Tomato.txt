White Beans with Roasted Tomato

For Beans:
1lb dried cannelloni beans (2c), picked over and rinsed
1 lb cipolline onion
1 ½ tsp salt

For tomatoes:
2 lb heirloom tomatoes, halved crosswise
1 lb cherry tomato
1 tsp salt
½ extra virgin olive oil
¼ c torn fresh basil

Cover beans with cold water by 2 inches in a bowl and soak at room temp at least 8 hrs. Drain well. Blanch onions in boiling salted water. Cover beans with cold water by about 1 inch in a 5 to 6 qt pot and bring to a boil. Add onions and simmer skim until beans preheated oven 500°F. Toss tomato halves and Cherry tomatoes with salt and oil in a shallow 3 qt baking dish.
Transfer beans and onions with a slotted spoon to a deep large platter; arrange tomatoes decoratively on top of beans and pour juices on top.   Sprinkle basil leaves.
