MISO MARINATED BLACK COD  
 
Prep time:15 minutes  
Cook time:7 minutes  
Yield:4 individual 4 ounce portions 
From: Chef Wolfgang Puck 
 
Ingredients: 
½ cup mirin 
¼ cup white miso paste 
1 tablespoon dark brown sugar 
1 tablespoon low­sodium soy sauce 
1 clove garlic, minced 
1 teaspoon ginger, minced 
4 (4­ounce) portions of black cod 
 
Method: 
In a small bowl, stir together the mirin, miso, sugar, soy sauce, garlic, and ginger. Add this marinade to the black cod fillets turning them to coat both sides. Cover the dish with plastic wrap and marinate in the refrigerator for at least 3 hour up to overnight.  
Preheat the broiler in your oven. Line a baking pan with aluminum foil. Place the fish on the baking tray in a single layer not touching. Place under the broiler and cook 5­7 minutes until nicely browned and cooked through. The fish should be rich in color and slightly charred.
