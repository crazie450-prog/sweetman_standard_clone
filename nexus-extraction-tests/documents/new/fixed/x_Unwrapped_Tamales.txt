Unwrapped Tamales

10-12 earscorn, kernels cut off (7 c corn)
1 c corn meal
¼ c butter (3 Tbsp will do room temp)
1 ½ tsp sugar
large pinch salt
Jack cheese

Place kernels in processor w/ cornmeal and grind add sugar, salt and butter. Line a greased casserole with ½ mixture lay on all cheese add as many pieces of chili as desired. Cover w/ remaining corn mixture. Stop and pack, bake 375 about 30 minutes. Cover with avocado slices and sour cream.
