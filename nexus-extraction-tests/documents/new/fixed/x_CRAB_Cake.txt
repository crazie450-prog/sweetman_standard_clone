CRAB CAKE 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Ingredients: 
2 slices bread, crusts removed 
2 tablespoons mayonnaise 
1 tablespoon lemon juice 
1 teaspoon sweet paprika  
2 teaspoons Old Bay Seasoning 
2 teaspoons parsley 
1 clove garlic, microplane 
1 teaspoons prepared yellow mustard 
1 egg, beaten 
 
1 pound lump crab meat 
 
Method: 
Break bread into crumb size pieces. Mix  mayonnaise, lemon juice, paprika, garlic,Old Bay, parsley, mustard, and egg. Combine all ingredients. Shape mixture into patties. Broil 10 minutes or fry until golden brown.  
  
prep time: 15 minutes  cook time:10 minutes  yield: 3 (6 ounce crab cakes)
