Pork Potsticker Filling

10 lb	pork butt cubed
4 T	Sea Salt
1 T 	Chicken Powder
¼ Cup	chopped garlic
¼	 Cup	chopped ginger
½	cup	sugar
¾ cup	oyster sauce
1	T	black pepper
1	T	white pepper
1	bunch	cilantro, chopped
1	bunch	scallions, chopped
3	T	sesame oil
3 T	Corn starch
1		cup	dried cherries
1		cup	dried apricots
1		cup	golden raisins


Place Cut pork for 1 HOUR to firm up 
Re-hydrate fruit in warm water, drain
Mix Together all the rest of the Seasonings in a large bowl
Grind Pork & Fruit on 1/4 die 
Combine Both the Pork with seasonings 
Place Pork in Hobart bowl and Paddle slowly then increase speed to Emulsify  


Potsticker Method

Place potsticker skin on cutting board.  Brush edges on half with egg yolk mixture (use 1 yolk and 1 whole egg).  Place dollop of filling in center of skin.  Form potsticker - see demo if needed.
