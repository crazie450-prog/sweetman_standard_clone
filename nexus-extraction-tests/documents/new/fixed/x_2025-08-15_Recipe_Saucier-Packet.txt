Cranberry Sauce (yields 30 gallons)

5 cases        Fresh Cranberries
4 cases        whole canned Cranberries (Ocean Spray)
1 gallon        Fresh Orange Juice
2 gallons      Orange Juice Concentrate 
1 gallon        Cranberry Juice (Ocean Spray)
60 lbs.         Sugar
2 each         Sachet  
30 each	Orange zest 

Sachet
2 cups whole Cardamom
2 cups whole Coriander
2 cups whole Allspice
2 cups whole cloves
3 oz. Cinnamon Sticks
8 cups chopped Ginger

Method

Steep Sachets at least 1 hour in Juices, maintain heat but do not boil.
Add Sugar, stir until totally dissolved.
Add fresh Cranberries and simmer until berries break down and mixture thickens.
Fold in zest and canned Cranberries. Bring back to temperature.
Season with Salt if needed.


NOTES:
1. 1 case cranberries = (24 each) 12 ounce packages
2. 1 case canned cranberries = (6 each) 6.5 ounce cans

Veal stock (Yields 32 - 36 gallons)

360 lbs.			Veal bones
2 x 400 Hotel pan		Onion (½" machine dice)
1 x 400 Hotel pan		Carrots (½" machine dice)
1 x 400 Hotel pan 		Celery (½" machine dice)
1 bag (6 oz.)	 		Thyme, fresh
15 each 			Bay leave
¼ cup 			Black Peppercorn
1 can (# 10)			Tomato paste

Method

Roast Veal bones at 365 Fahrenheit for 2 ¾ hours until nice mahogany color.
Saute Onions and Carrots until well done and strong caramelized without burned fond. Add Tomato paste and caramelize until a rich red-brown color is achieved. Add Celery and cook until liquid is dissolved.
Place Bones into Kettle and cover well with Water and bring to boil. Boil for 2 minutes and reduce temperature to a light simmer. Degrasse and add Mire poix, Thyme and Pepper. Simmer for 24 - 27 hours. Strain through Chinois.  


Demi Glace

Reduce 1 x Recipe Veal stock to 12 gallons.
Place Veal stock in 35 gallon Swiss Bracer, put bucket under the pour spout in case it boils over. Reduce Veal stock slowly. To measure Quantity place 
24 oz. Ladle in Tilt Skillet, the Demi will be at the top rim when 10 gallons is achieved. Degrasse and drain through Chinois.   








Remoulage (yields 20 - 25 gallons)

After Veal stock is drained keep Bones in Kettle and cover with Water. Bring temperature back to a boil then reduce heat. Let simmer for another 
24 - 27 hours. Strain through Chinois.


Glace de viande (yields 3 - 4 gallons)

Fill Remoulage into 25 gallon Swiss Bracer, put bucket under the pour spout in case it boils over. Reduce Remoulage slowly. As the Stock approaches the last 3rd to 4th of its reduction, it'll create a small bubble foam for a short time and then die off. When the Glace is close to completion the bubbles will be small and tight against the liquid, then a patch of a large bubble will form (it's done). The glace should run off the spoon in a couple of drips but coat the spoon moderately.

Roux (yields 4 gallons)

2 gallons	Sterling oil
25 lbs. 	All Purpose Flour

Method

Choose a wide Pot. Heat the oil on moderate flat top heat. When the oil is lightly wavy, add the flour while stirring. Reduce the heat and stir frequently. Cook about 20 - 25 minutes.

Note:
All Recipes in this book are based on this ratio.


Brown Roux (yields 4 gallons)

2 gallons 	Sterling oil
25 lbs. 	All Purpose Flour

Method

Choose wide Pot. Heat the oil on low flat top heat. Mix about 7 - 8 lbs. of the flour into the oil until a sauce like consistency is achieved. Stir frequently. Cook about 6 hours while stirring frequently. The roux should be deep, rich, and mahagony in color. When color is achieved gradually add the remaining flour. Cool the roux in the same pot overnight.
  
If the bottom burns:
Stir the roux well, find the burn spot and avoid this area. Strain through a chinois into a new pot and proceed.
If the burn is heavy, oh well start again.

4 gallons		Heavy Cream

4 Cups		Cornstarch slurry (Heavy Cream consistency)
2 cups		Salt and Pepper mix

Method

Cool the cans of Coconut milk for at least 24 hours in walk-in cooler, that the fat can separate from the milk.

Combine the Oxtail meat, Hominy, Tomatoes and Tomato soup and simmer for 30-40 minutes. Adjust the seasoning if necessary.

Smoking Salmon
Vessels:
A: 1 Fryer Basket
B: 1 Speed Rack
C: 6 cups Wood chips
D: 8 pc. Charcoal
E: 1 Body bag
	F: 1 Apple wood log
G: 3 each Sheet trays
H: 2 each 2" Roasting pans
I: Ice cubes
J: Plastic wrap
 	
Method
Set up the Speed rack with Salmon, Sheet tray on the bottom as under layer for the Fryer basket, Sheet trays with Roasting pans filled with Ice cubes above. Leave an acceptable air gap in between to let the fire breath and the Ice cool the smoke.     
Place 4 pc. Charcoal on Burner until it burns almost white, add a log of Apple wood above until it starts to burn.
Place 2 pc. Charcoal on the bottom of the Fryer, 3 cups Woodchips on top and the other 2 pc. Charcoal on top of that, finish the stack with the log of Apple wood.
Place the Fryer basket on the bottom of the Speed rack. 
Now cover the Speed rack with the Body bag put in a small hole on the top, as a vent and wrap on the bottom around with plastic wrap.
The smoke should be clearly evident but not to be white or cloudy after about 30-40 min. After 3 hours the process should be repeated. Total smoking time is 6 hours

½ x 200 Hotel pan		Celery, ½" machine dice

3 oz.				Thyme, fresh
¼ cup				Black Peppercorn
8 each 			Bay leaves

Method

Roast 40 lbs Lamb shanks, 20 lbs Lamb bones and 3 lbs. Lamb meat scraps
at 365 Fahrenheit for 2 hours until nice mahogany color.
Saute Onions and Carrots in the Bacon fat until well done and strong caramelized without burned fond. Add Tomato paste and caramelize until
a rich red-brown color is achieved. Add Celery and cook until liquid is dissolved.
Cover raw Lamb shank slices well with Water and bring to boil, than add roasted Shanks, Bones and Scraps. Boil for 1-2 minutes then reduce temperature to a light simmer. Degrasse and add Mire poix, Thyme,
Bay leaves and Pepper. Simmer for 16 hours. Strain through a Chinois.

Onion relish (yields ~ 8 gallons)

100 lbs. 			Yellow Onions, sliced
100 lbs.			Vidalia Onions, sliced
2 gallons			Apple Cider Vinegar (Heinz)
2 gallons			Sugar
2 cups			Thyme, fresh chopped
2/3 x 200 Hotel pan	double smoked Slab Bacon, small diced
3½ cups			Salt & Pepper mix

Method

Roast diced Bacon in 2" Roasting pan at 350 degrees for 23 min. until crispy, strain fat out.
Place some Water and Oil in a Swiss braiser add Onions and cook on moderate heat. Do not let the Onions take any color.
If an excessive amount of Onion juice is produced, strain it out and continue cooking.
After about 3-4 hours of cooking time, when the Onions are cooked down well and begin to take on color add the Sugar, Vinegar, Salt & Pepper mix and Bacon.
Cook everything down sec with a nice mahogany color than add the Thyme and mix well. Let cool, cap and store in cooler.















Sweet Pea Coulis (yields 1 gallon)

¼ gallon	Chicken stock
¼ gallon	Clam juice
¾ gallon	Sweet Peas, frozen
1 each	Onion, large dice
1 each	Ham hock 
¾ cup	Butter
¼ cup	Salt & Pepper mix

Method

Steep, but not simmering, Chicken stock, Clam juice, Onions and Ham hock for 3½ hours. Retrieve the Ham hock and bring the Stock to a boil, add the peas and cook until the peas just begin to shrivel and the shade turns just a little gray. Remove some of the Stock that the liquid level is obviously below the level of peas. Save the extra liquid to adjust if necessary.
Add the Butter and Salt & Pepper mix, burr mix completely, strain through a chinois, cool, cap and store in cooler.

5 cups	Kosher Salt
5 cups	Sugar
6 oz.	Thyme, fresh
¼ cup	Black Peppercorn
2 bulb	Garlic, split

Method

Bring Water, Salt and Sugar to a boil.
Split Lemons into 2 Buckets (5 gallon), place in each Bucket

Asparagus Consommé (yields 5 gallons)

5 gallons 	Chicken stock
1½ gallons  	Ice, crushed
5 gallons	Asparagus, trim or whole
50 each	Egg whites
5 lbs. 	Chicken meat, lean
4 pc.	Onion brulee
1 cup	Salt & Pepper mix
1 each	Sachet (1 can (#5) white Asparagus drained and wrapped in Coffee filter, then in Cheesecloth)

Method
 
 Grind Asparagus and Chicken meat, add Egg whites and crushed Ice. Mix thoroughly and season with Salt and Pepper, add to cold Chicken stock in a tall thin pot, add Onion brulee. 1 cup			Mustard Seed, whole
1 cup			Celery Seed, whole

Method

Slice the Cucumbers and mix well with the Kosher Salt, under refrigeration let them stand for 2 hours to extract excessive Water.

Green Tomato Salsa (yields 3-3½ gallons)

20 lbs.	Tomatillos, cleaned and quartered or halved (depending on size)
1 cup		Sugar
1 cup		Salt & Pepper mix
1¾ cup 		Extra Virgin Olive Oil
5 lbs.		Anaheim Peppers, split and seeded
5 each		Onions, small diced
3 cups		Garlic, chopped
2½ cups		Rice Wine Vinegar
1 can (46 oz)		Pineapple juice, unsweetened (no Sugar added)
10 lbs.		Yellow Tomatoes, outside flesh only
2 bunch		Cilantro, stems removed, chopped
2 each		Lemons, zest
4 each 		Lemons, juice
½ cup		Sambal paste
½ cup		Cumin, ground, toasted
1/3 cup		Coriander, ground
1/3 cup		Light Red Chili Powder

Method

Mix the Tomatillos with 1 cup Olive Oil, Sugar and Salt & Pepper mix well, divide them Tomatillos into 2 x 2" Roasting pans and place them for 
13 minutes at 425 degrees in the Oven, cool completely.
Char only the inside flesh of the Anaheim Peppers partially, don't over char, then small dice them (1/3").
Lightly caramelize the Onions and Garlic, add the Pineapple juice, Rice Wine Vinegar and diced Peppers and reduce to sec, cool completely on a Sheet tray.
Dice the outside flesh of Tomatoes, small to medium dice, chop Cilantro, Zest and Juice the Lemons.
In a large bowl fold together the Tomatillos, Onion & Pepper mix, Tomatoes, Cilantro, Lemon juice and zest, Spices and remaining Olive Oil.
If necessary re season with Salt & Pepper mix and or Sugar.
