Jalapeno Vodka
Ingredients
 3 jalapeño peppers, stems trimmed 
 1 bottle (750 milliliters) vodka or blanco (silver) tequila 
Directions:  Place peppers directly over a gas flame (or on a rack beneath a broiler) and roast until charred all over. Let cool, then transfer to a resealable container. Add vodka or tequila, seal, and let infuse in a cool, dry place, until flavored to desired intensity, about 1 to 3 days. Strain vodka or tequila through a coffee filter into a clean jar or resealable container. 

Jalapeno Cucumber Basil Martini
Makes 2 drinks 
Ingredients
 2 Tbsp. simple syrup 
 4 large basil leaves 
 ¼ cup roasted-jalapeño-infused vodka 
 ½ cup fresh cucumber juice (see note, below) 
 1 Tbsp. fresh lime juice 
 Cucumber slices, for garnish 
Note: To make cucumber juice, use a juice extractor. Alternatively, peel and chop ½ large cucumber and place in a blender or a food processor fitted with a metal blade. Puree, then strain through a fine-mesh strainer. 
Directions
Total time: 20 minutes (plus 1 to 3 days for infusing vodka) 

Place simple syrup and basil in a cocktail shaker and muddle the basil, gently pressing and twisting to release its oils. 

Add vodka, cucumber juice, and lime juice and fill shaker with ice. Shake vigorously until very cold and strain into 2 chilled martini glasses. Garnish with cucumber slices and serve. 

Watermelon – Mint – Martini 

Makes 2 drinks 
Ingredients
 6 mint leaves, plus sprigs for garnish 
 1 Tbsp. simple syrup (see directions below) 
 1 Tbsp. fresh lemon juice 
 1 cup watermelon cubes 
 1/2 cup vodka 
Directions

Total time: 20 minutes 

To make simple syrup: Combine 1 cup granulated sugar with 1 cup water in a glass jar; seal tightly. Shake until dissolved. Refrigerate until needed. 

Place mint leaves, 1 Tbsp. simple syrup, and lemon juice in a cocktail shaker; muddle the mint, pressing and twisting to release its oils. Add watermelon cubes and muddle until they're juiced. 

Fill shaker with ice and shake vigorously until very cold. Strain into 2 chilled martini glasses. Garnish with mint sprigs and serve.

Apple – Thyme – Martini 

Ingredients
  1/4 cup vodka
  2 tablespoons green apple syrup 
  1/4 cup cloudy apple juice  
  1 tablespoon lemon juice
  1 tablespoon thyme syrup
  1/2 tablespoon Elderflower cordial
  Cubed ice
  2 sprigs thyme (for garnish) 
To Make Thyme Syrup
 1 cup   water
 1 cup superfine sugar
 6 sprigs Thyme
Directions
To make thyme syrup: Place water, sugar and 6 thyme sprigs in a saucepan over high heat and stir to dissolve sugar. Bring to the boil and cook for 3 minutes. Remove from heat, allow to cool and discard thyme sprigs. Makes 1 cup.

To make Apple and Thyme Martini: Place the vodka, apple syrup, apple juice, lemon juice, thyme syrup and elderflower cordial in a cocktail shaker with ice cubes and shake. Strain into a cocktail glass and garnish with thyme sprigs. Makes 2.   

Habanero Passion Fruit Martini 
make this drink extra-spicy by infusing vodka with roasted habanero peppers instead of jalapeños (just substitute 2 habaneros for the 3 jalapeños in the infusion recipe).

Makes 2 drinks 
Ingredients
 Granulated sugar, for rimming glasses
 ¼ cup agave syrup 
 ¼ cup roasted-jalapeño-infused vodka 
 ½ cup fresh passion fruit puree, strained, or guava juice 

Directions
Total time: 20 minutes (plus 1 to 3 days for infusing vodka) 

Wet rims of 2 chilled martini glasses and roll in sugar to coat. 

In a cocktail shaker, combine agave, vodka and passion fruit puree. Fill shaker with ice and shake vigorously until very cold. Strain into glasses and serve.
