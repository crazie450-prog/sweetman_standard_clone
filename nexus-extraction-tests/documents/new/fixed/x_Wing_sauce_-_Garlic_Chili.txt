Sheet: Sheet1
	wolfgang puck bar and grill
	recipe name:  garlic-lemon-chili wing sauce
	yield:  1 1/2 quarts
qty.	product
2 c	garlic, chopped
1 c	shallot, chopped
3 c	lemon juice
1 c	white wine
1/2 c	rice wine vinegar
1 1/2 c	sugar
1 T	chili flake
2 c	chicken stock
2 T	olive oil blend
1 t	salt
1/2 t	cracked black pepper
	method:
1	on low, low, low, low heat caramelize shallots and garlic with oil
2	add sugar and stir until lightly caramelized
3	add lemon juice, white wine and vinegar and turn heat to medium
4	reduce by one half, add chicken stock
5	reduce to a glaze consistency
6	add chili, salt, sugar
7	cool
