PASTA PRIMAVERA 
 
Prep time: 20
Cook time: 15
Yield: portions
 
Ingredients: 
Montelbllo strozzapreti pasta | 5 cups
Water | 1 gallon
Sea salt | 2 tablespoons
Olive oil | 2 teaspoons
Shallot, fine dice | 1 medium
Garlic, minced | 3 medium
Crushed red chili flake | 1 teaspoon
Carrots, fine dice, blanched | 3 medium
Zucchini, medium dice, blanched | 1 medium
Crimini mushrooms, cut in 1/6ths, roasted | 6 medium
Pomi chopped tomatoes | 26.4 ounces
Yellow bell pepper, small dice | 1 medium
Broccoli, dime size florets, blanched | 1 cup
Parmesan cheese, grated | 2 tablespoons
Basil, chiffonade
Black pepper
Sea Salt
Basil, puree
Pine nuts, toasted
 
 
Method: 
Bring a large pot of water to boil.  Add 2 tablespoon salt and cook pasta until al dente. 
Meanwhile, in a large non­stick skillet over medium heat, warm the oil.  Add the shallots and garlic.  Cook and stir for 2 minutes.  Add the rest of the vegetables and cook, stirring frequently for 5 minutes, or until the vegetables are hot. Add 6 cups of the cooked pasta to the vegetable mix. Add the tomato juce from the diced tomatoes and allow pasta to get heated through. Remove the pasta from the heat and add parmesan cheese and adjust seasoning with salt and pepper.. Divide the pasta prima vera between 6 bowls and top with basil puree,  basil chifonnade and toasted pine nuts.  Cook for 2 minutes until warm.
