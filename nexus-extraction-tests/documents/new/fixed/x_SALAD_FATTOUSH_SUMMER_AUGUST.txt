Fattoush salad: a refreshing Middle Eastern bread salad
Fattoush is a popular and flavorful Lebanese salad that originated in the Levant region. It is a "bread salad" that traditionally incorporates stale or leftover pita bread. This practice reflects the resourcefulness of Lebanese cooking 
Fattoush Salad
Prep time: 15
Cook time: 10 minutes
Yield: 6 petite or 3 side salads
Credit - Heidi Insalata Krahling

Ingredients:
pita bread chips
vinaigrette
salad

Pita Bread Chips
pita breads					
Method:
Preheat oven to 350* F. Trim the edges off the pita breads, keeping a circle shape, then split horizontally into two halves. Cut each half into 6 triangles and arrange on baking sheet. Toast in 350* F. oven until gold and crispy, about 10 minutes. Allow to cool. Break the chips into large pieces. 

Vinaigrette
blended oil					⅓ cup
garlic, minced					1 teaspoon
freshly squeezed lemon juice			¼ cup
EVOO 						½ cup
kosher salt 					1 teaspoon
cumin seed, toasted and ground 		⅛ teaspoon

Method:
In a small skillet, over low heat, gently warm the oil blend and garlic until fragrant. In a medium bowl, whisk together the oil and garlic with the lemon juice, olive oil, salt, cumin and pepper. Taste and adjust seasoning as needed. 

Salad:
hearts of romaine, torn roughly by hand		3 hearts, or roughly six cups
sheep’s milk feta cheese, crumbled 		             ¾ cup
cherry tomatoes, vine ripened, halved		¾ cup
English cucumber, peeled, seeded, and diced 	¾ cup
Kalamata olives, pitted 				⅓ cup
red onion, thinly sliced 				¼ cup
cilantro, finely chopped				⅓ cup
mint, finely chopped 				              ⅓ cup
Sumac, to garnish                                                                  
Method:
To serve, combine salad ingredients with pita chips and ¾ cup vinaigrette and toss well, adding more vinaigrette if needed to coat the leaves. Divide the salad among 3 or 6 chilled salad plates.
