Shrimp Har Gow for Spring Roll

13# Shrimp (Peel, Devein, Remove Tails), Blot Dry Thoroughly
4.5 oz Salt (Sea, Weighed)
10 oz Sugar
1.5oz Chicken Powder (Knorr)
1 T White Pepper (Ground Fine)
¾ Cup Corn Starch
10 oz Chicken Fat (Cold Solid)
10 oz Pork Fat back Small Diced

Method:
 Cut each shrimp into 3 pieces
 Place everything in Hobart
 Paddle all cut shrimp in Hobart on low for 1 min
 Scrap bowl and add all remaining ingredients
 Paddle on high for 1 min until emulsified
