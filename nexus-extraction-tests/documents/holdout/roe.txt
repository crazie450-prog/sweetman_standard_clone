Roe, Smoked Steelhead, and Cured Char with Buckwheat Crepe and Sour Cream

⅓ tsp active dry yeast
⅓ c warm milk 110°F
1 large egg
2 tsp sugar
⅓ tsp kosher salt
2/4 c AP flour
1 c cold whole milk
⅔ c clarified butter

6 slices sliced smoked whitefish
6 slices sliced smoked steelhead
6 slices sliced home-cured steelhead

Sour Cream
1 oz steelhead roe
⅓ bunch chives, thinly sliced

Buckwheat Crepe Batter: sprinkle yeast over warm milk and stir to dissolve. Separate the egg and put the yolk in a large bowl. Cover and refrigerate the white. Add sugar, salt, 1/4 c AP flour, and 1/4 c buckwheat flour to the yolk and whisk . Add yeast mixture and mix until smooth, cover with plastic, put in a warm place until doubled in volume. Add the cold milk and remaining 1/4 c AP flour and 1/4 c buckwheat flour whisk until smooth. Let it rise again for 19 min. To cook buckwheat crepe, beat egg white until stiff peak foam. Gently fold in buckwheat crepe batter. In a nonstick pan add clarified butter and let it get warm and add some of the batter, when the side is light brown flip to cook other side.
