Miniature Caramelized Onion Tartlets

Yield:  24 tartlets

Basic Pie Dough

Caramelized Onions
2 Tbsp butter
2 medium-size onions, preferably Vidalia, very thinly sliced
1 t sugar

Custard:
5 eggs
¾ c heavy cream
2 Tbsp Dijon mustard
Pinch of celery salt
Pinch of cayenne pepper

Tartlets:
Lightly spray 2 gem pans or mini muffin tins with nonstick cooking spray.
Roll the dough out evenly on a floured board to about 1/8 inch thick.  Using a 3-inch round cookie cutter, stamp out 24 circles.  Line the inside of each muffin cup with a dough round, using your fingertips to press it into shape.
Fill each cup with pie weights or dried beans and chill for 30 minutes.
Preheat the over to 350 degrees
Bake the shells for 6 minutes.  Remove from the oven and cool in the pans
When the shells are cool, invert the pans onto a baking sheet.  Pick out any remaining pie weights or beans and save for future use.

Caramelized Onions:
In a heavy-bottomed saucepan, melt the butter over medium heat.  Add the onion slices and cook, stirring, until wilted.  Reduce the heat and cook, stirring frequently, until the onions develop a rich golden color.
Add the sugar and continue cooking until the onions are the color of brown sugar.  Remove from the heat and set aside.

Custard:
In a small bowl, whisk together the eggs, cream, mustard, celery salt and cayenne.

Assembly
Preheat the oven to 350 degrees
Place ½ t of the caramelized onions in each pastry shell.  Cover the onions with the custard
Place the tartlets on a wire rack set on a baking sheet.  Bake for 10-minutes or until the custard is just set.
