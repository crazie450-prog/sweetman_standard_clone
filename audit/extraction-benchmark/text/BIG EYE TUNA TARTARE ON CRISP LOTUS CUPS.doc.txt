BIG EYE TUNA TARTARE ON CRISP LOTUS CUPS


Season to taste one pound of high quality, Raw Tuna with salt and pepper.  Chop the tuna very finely into small cubes.

Dress with the Soy-Lime Vinaigrette (see below.)
Peel one Lotus Root and slice it thinly into round slices.  Fry in peanut oil at 350 degrees until crisp. (About 30 seconds.)
Garnish with Fried Nori (Seaweed) and one ounce of Black Caviar.
(optional) Serve with chilled Cucumber and Red Onion Salad


SOY-LIME VINAIGRETTE

½ 	cup		Soy Sauce
½	cup		Lime Juice
½ 	cup		Peanut oil
1/8	cup		Rice wine vinegar
1	each		Scallions
2 or 3 	cloves		Garlic
2 or 3 	each		Shallots
1	each		Ginger root
1	each		Jalapeño pepper

METHOD:

Cut vegetables into large pieces.
Combine all ingredients.
Let sit until flavors combine.
Strain.
