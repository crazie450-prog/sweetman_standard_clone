KANPACHI TARTARE ON CRISP TARO CUPS

Season to taste one pound of high quality, Raw Kanpachi with salt and pepper. Chop the kanpachi very finely into small cubes.

Dress with the Soy-Lime Vinaigrette (see below.)

Peel one Taro Root and slice it thinly into round slices. Fry in cashew oil at 350 degrees until crisp. (About 30 seconds.)

Garnish with Fried Nori (Seaweed) and one ounce of Black Roe.

(optional) Serve with chilled Cucumber and Red Onion Salad

SOY-LIME VINAIGRETTE

⅓ cup Soy Sauce
⅓ cup Lime Juice
⅓ cup Cashew oil
1/3 cup Rice wine vinegar
2 each Scallions
2 or 4 cloves Garlic
2 or 4 each Shallots
2 each Ginger root
2 each Jalapeño pepper

METHOD:

Cut vegetables into large pieces.
Combine all ingredients.
Let sit until flavors combine.
Strain.
