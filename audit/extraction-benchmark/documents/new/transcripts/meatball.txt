Flow Chart for World's Best Meatball Recipe
1. Prepare the Meatball Mixture:
- Soak breadcrumbs & milk (2 min)
- Add ground beef, pork, Parmesan, parsley, egg, garlic, oregano, salt, pepper
- Gently mix by hand (don't overmix)
2. Form Meatballs:
- Roll mixture into 1.5-inch meatballs
3. Sear the Meatballs:
- Heat olive oil in skillet
- Sear meatballs in batches until brown on all sides
4. Make the Tomato Sauce:
- Sauté onion in olive oil (5 min)
- Add garlic (30 sec)
- Add tomatoes, basil, salt, pepper, sugar
- Simmer 10 min
5. Combine and Cook:
- Add meatballs to sauce
- Simmer 20-25 min, covered
6. Serve:
- Serve over pasta, polenta, or alone
- Garnish with Parmesan and basil
