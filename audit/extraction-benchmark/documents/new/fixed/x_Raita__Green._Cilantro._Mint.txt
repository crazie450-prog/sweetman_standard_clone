Cilantro Mint Raita
 
1Cup Whole Milk Yogurt
1Cup Crème Fraiche
1/4 CupHoney
1 Cup Cilantro (Chiffonade)
1 Cup Mint (Chiffonade)
2 Japanese Cucumbers (Peeled &Grated)
1/2 Medium Red Onion (Grated)
Salt
Black Pepper
1 T Red Vinegar
1 T Lemon Juice
1 T Garam Masala

Puree All Ingredients Together in High Speed Blender until Green and Smooth
