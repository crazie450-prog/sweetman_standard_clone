Tuna Tartare with Herb Salad and Ginger Vinaigrette

1 lb sushi-grade yellow fin tuna, well chilled
6 shiso leaves, fine chiffonade
1/3 c thinly sliced scallions, white only
½ c ginger vinaigrette
tt coarse salt and white pepper

slice tuna ¼- inch dice, cover and refrigerate, before serving mix tuna, shiso leaves, scallions, and vinaigrette, season with salt and pepper.

Herb Salad:
1 small head baby frisee, rinsed, dried, and torn into 1 ½ to 1 inch pieces
½ c gently packed fresh chervil leaves
1/3 c gently packed fresh parsley leaves
1/3 c gently packed fresh cilantro leaves
¼ pkg dikon sprouts

Ginger Vinaigrette:
1/3 c lime juice
3 Tbsp fresh ginger juice
1/8 tsp hot red pepper sauce, Tabasco
1 Tbsp minced shallot
1 garlic clove minced and mashed to a paste
tt coarse salt and white pepper

Whisk together all ingredients.
