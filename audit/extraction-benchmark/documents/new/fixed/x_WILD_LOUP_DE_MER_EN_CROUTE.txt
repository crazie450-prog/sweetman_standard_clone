WILD LOUP DE MER ''EN CROUTE''  


Fishmousse- grind 8oz of skinless black bass on fine plate, freeze for 20 min.
In food processor puree fish, 1 cup cream, 1 egg white, salt, pepper until smooth,
Do not overwork. On ice add cooked vegetable brunoise ( carrot, leeks; onion, celery )
And chopped, sautéed black trumpet mushrooms, chives, parsley, tarragon, dill.
Cut puff pastry sheet into rectangles, place a 4 oz piece of skinless loup de mer on 
Top of pastry, season with salt and pepper, pipe 3 oz of fish mousse on top, cover with
An other piece of pastry, brush with egg wash and bake at 375◦ for 15 min.
Serve with seasonal vegetables and a beurre blanc sauce.
