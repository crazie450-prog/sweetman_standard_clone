Texas Barbecued Beef Brisket

1 10-to 12 lbs beef Brisket
2 c All-South Barbecue Rub
2 c Basic All-American Barbecue sauce

Rub Brisket with Barbecue rub, and allow to come to room temp.
Place brisket on grill, and cook

Sauces:
Mexican
1 tsp ground
1 tsp chili powder
4 Tbsp lime juice
10 cilantro springs

Asian
2 tsp minced ginger
8 Tbsp soy sauce
4 Tbsp rice wine vinegar
2 Tbsp sugar
1 Tbsp sesame oil

Caribbean
2 tsp brown sugar
4 Tbsp pineapple juice
4 Tbsp dark rum
4 Tbsp Caribbean hot sauce
Juice of 1 orange
Pinch of allspice
