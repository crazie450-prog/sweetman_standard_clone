Sheet: Tuna Sashimi
Yellowfin Tuna Sashimi and Tartare Salad		serves 4
yellowfin tuna sashimi	28	slices
yellowfin tuna loin, 1/4" diced	4	oz
avocado, 1/4" diced	1	ea
scallions, fine julienne	2	ea
carrot, fine julienne	0.25	ea
japanese cucumber, fine julienne	1	ea
red endive, fine julienne	1	ea
kaiware sprouts	1	oz
beet, fine julienne	1	ea
after cutting the vegetables, soak all except for the beets in ice water for 5 minutes and then drain.		
soak and drain beet julienne separately, then mix in with other vegetables.		
		
pickled ginger, julienne	1	oz
soy-yuzu dressing		recipe below
asian vinaigrette		recipe below
salt and pepper		to taste
To Plate		
Mix diced tuna and avocado.  Divide equally and place in center of 4 plates		
Arrange slices of tuna sashimi around tuna/avocado mixture.		
Mix julienne vegetables with 2 oz of asian vinaigrette.  Season with salt and pepper.		
Place julienne vegetables on top of tuna/avocado mixture.  Top with julienne pickled ginger for garnish.		
Spoon the soy-yuzu sauce between the pieces of sashimi.		
Soy-Yuzu Dressing
yuzu zest	0.5	cup
shallots, fine diced	0.25	cup
ginger, minced	0.25	cup
green onions, minced	0.5	cup
fresh wasabi	0.25	cup
sambal	2	tb
yuzu juice	0.75	cup
kikoman soy sauce	1	cup
pickled ginger, minced	0.3333333333333333	cup
sesame oil	0.25	cup
extra virgin olive oil	0.5	cup
Mix well in bowl.  Store in refrigerator.		
Asian Vinaigrette
ginger vinegar	1	cup
chili oil	1	tb
sesame oil	2	tb
peanut oil	1	cup
salt	1	tsp
white pepper	1	tsp
toasted sesame seeds, white and black	2	tb
Mix well in bowl.  Store in refrigerator.  Shake well before using.		

Sheet: Steamed Mussels
Steamed Mussels with Thai Coconut Broth		serves 2
farm raised mussels, cleaned	20	ea
thai coconut broth	0.75	cup, recipe below
fresh thai chilies, cut in thing rings	3	ea
sliced garlic, lighlty blanched	2	cloves
red bell pepper, diced	1	ea
scallions, rings thinly sliced	2	ea
In a large saute pan, saute the garlic and thai chilies in small amount of butter.		
Add the mussels, and heat slightly.  Add the coconut broth.  Cover.		
Cook until all the mussels have opened.  Mix in the diced pepper.		
Pour in a bowl, garlish with scallions.		
		
		
Thai Coconut Broth
fresh ginger, smashed	1	oz
peanut oil	2	tb
lemongrass stalk, smashed	1	ea
thai basil, whole leaves	0.25	cup
fresh thai chilis	5	ea
garlic, smashed	5	cloves
shallots, chopped	2	ea
coconut milk, canned	5	14 oz cans
galangal, smashed	1	oz
fish sauce	2	tb
lemon juice	1	tb
sugar	2	tb
kaffir lime leaf	0.25	cup
salt and pepper		to taste
Heat peanut oil in sauce pan.  Add ginger, garlic, thai chilis, shallots, and galangal.  Sweat for 5 minutes.		
Add coconut milk, lemongrass, fish sauce, lemon juice, sugar, salt and pepper, and keffir lime leaf.  Simmer for 15 minutes.		
Remove from heat, strain through fine chinois, cool.		
		
		

Sheet: Wild Salmon Tandoori
Wild Salmon Tandoori		serves 4
wild salmon pieces, 2 oz ea, skinless	8	ea
tandoori marinade	2	cups, recipe below
allow salmon to marinate in tandoori marinade for 6 hours.		
pickled cucumbers		recipe below
cilantro mint chutney		recipe below
scallions, fine julienne	2	ea
garam masala		pinch
Cover a baking pan with aluminum foil and butter the foil slightly.  Place the marinated salmon on the pan and put into a 400 degree oven.					
Cook until medium, basting with excess marinade.					
Place a small amount of cilantro-mint chutney on plate, in two circles.					
Place a small amount of pickled cucumbers in center of each circle.					
Place a piece of salmon on each pile of cucumbers.					
Top each piece of salmon with julienne scallions.					
Garnish plate with a few sprinkles of garam masala		
		
Pickled Cucumbers
japanese cucumbers, peeled and sliced into rings	4	ea
salt	0.5	tb
yogurt	2	tb
chopped dill	0.5	tsp
caraway seed, cracked	0.5	tsp
salt and pepper		to taste
rice vinegar	1	tb
sugar	1	tb
Put cucumbers and 1/2 tb salt in bowl.  Mix.  Allow to sit for hour.  Drain off water.		
Mix remaining ingredients in bowl.  Add cucumbers.  Marinate a couple of hours before serving.		
		
Tandoori Marinade
paprika	0.5	cup
turmeric	0.25	cup
cayenne	0.25	cup
coriander	0.5	cup
cumin	0.25	cup
ground ginger	2	tb
ground cardamon	0.75	cup
nutmeg	1	cup
black pepper	0.3333333333333333	cup
sea salt	0.25	cup
Grind all in spice grinder.  Then add to blender and add the following		
		
garlic	2	cloves
tamarind paste	1	tb
honey	0.75	cup
yogurt	0.75	cup
Blend until smooth.  Keep in refrigerator.
Cilantro-Mint Chutney
coconut milk	1	cup
shredded coconut, sweetened	1	tb
Heat coconut milk slighty, add coconut.  Remove from heat.  Then add to blender and add the following.		
garlic	3	cloves
yogurt	4	oz
honey	1	oz
cayenne	0.25	tsp
cumin	1	tb
coriander	1	tb
fennugreek	1	tsp
sugar	1	tb
salt and pepper		to taste
Puree in blender.  Allow to cool.  Then add the following in the blender.		
cilantro leaves	2	cup
mint leaves	1.5	cup
lemon juice	0.5	cup
Blend until smooth.  Keep in refrigerator.		

Sheet: Whole Fried Striped Bass
Whole Fried Wild Striped Bass		serves 2
whole wild striped bass, 2 lb, scaled and gutted	1	ea
ginger, thinly sliced	10	ea
cilantro leaves	30	ea
peanut oil for frying
salt and pepper		to taste
cornstarch
ponzu sauce	1	cup, recipe follows
cilantro sprigs	1	ea
scallions, fine julienne	1	ea
Make 5 vertical slices in each side of the striped bass, being careful to leave it attached to the bone.  Stuff 1 slice of ginger and 3 cilantro leaves in each slice.		
Season the fish with salt and pepper.  Slightly dust the entire fish with cornstarch.		
Heat oil in large wok or stock pot to 350 degrees.  Place fish in oil.		
Cook for about 8 minutes, or until cooked through.		
Drain, place on plate belly side down.  Garnish top of fish with scallions and cilantro.		
Serve with ponzu sauce.		
		
Ponzu Sauce
kikoman soy sauce	1.5	cup
yuzu juice	1	tb
rice vinegar	1	cup
mirin	2	cup
lemon, cut in half	1	cup
lemongrass stalk, cut and smashed	1	ea
ginger, smashed	1	piece
jalapeno, smashed	1	ea
Mix well in bowl.  Store in refrigerator.		

Sheet: Miso Glazed Black Cod
Miso Glazed Black Cod		serves 4
black cod pieces, 2 oz ea, skinless	8	ea
miso marinade	1	cup, recipe below
mix marinade with black cod and allow to marinate for 12 hours					
scallions, fine julienne	2	ea
carrot, fine julienne	0.25	ea
micro pea shoots	3	oz
asian vinaigrette	2	oz, recipe below
sesame miso vinaigrette		recipe below
Cover a baking pan with aluminum foil and butter the foil slightly.  Place the marinated black cod on the pan and put into a 400 degree oven.					
Cook until medium, basting with excess marinade.					
Place a small amount of sesame miso vinaigrette in the center of each plate.					
Place 2 pieces of cooked black cod in the center of the vinaigrette.					
Mix scallions, carrots, and micro pea shoots with asian vinaigrette, place on top of each piece of cod.					
Sesame-Miso Vinaigrette
egg yolk	2	ea
dijon mustard	2	tb
garlic cloves	2	ea
pickled ginger	0.25	cup
red miso paste	0.25	cup
white sesame seeds, toasted	0.25	cup
ginger vinegar	2	tb
rice vinegar	0.5	cup
Place in blender, blend until smooth.  Slowly add the following.					
peanut oil	2	cup
sesame oil	2	tb
togarashi spice	1	tsp
Add oil slowly, until fully emulsified.
Asian Vinaigrette
ginger vinegar	1	cup
chili oil	1	tb
sesame oil	2	tb
peanut oil	1	cup
salt	1	tsp
white pepper	1	tsp
toasted sesame seeds, white and black	2	tb
Mix well in bowl.  Store in refrigerator.  Shake well before using.
