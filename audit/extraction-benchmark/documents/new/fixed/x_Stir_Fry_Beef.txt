Stir-Fry Filet of Beef with Chili Garlic & Sweet Bean Sauce
Golden Chives, Spring Peas, Eggplant, Fried Baby Spinach


6 Oz Filet (Cut into ¼ inch by 3 inch strips)
1 ½ Oz Beef Velvet



Beef Velvet:
1 Cup Soy superior
1 Cup Mushroom Soy
¾ Cup Corn Starch
1 ½ Cup Brown Sugar (Light) Packed
¾ Cup Chili Goop
¾ Cup Chili Oil


Method:
Combine all ingredients into large mixing bowl and whisk until emulsified.  Coat portioned beef with velvet and marinade for at least thirty minutes.



 Hot Sweet Bean Sauce
½ C "Lee Kum Kee" Chili Bean Sauce
1/3 C House Hoisin
1/3 C Sugar
2/3 C Rice Wine
2 C Double Stock
2 T  Wok Sambal
¼ C Soy Mix


Method:
Mix all ingredients together thoroughly.    








House Hoisin Special Sauce


1 Cup Hoisin (Kcun Chin)
1 T Soy
1 T Sugar
1 T Water
1 T Sweet Bean Paste
2 t Rice Vinegar
½ t Mala Oil


Method:
Mix all ingredients and heat in sauté pan until mixture comes to a low simmer.  Pull from heat and add Mala Oil.  



Mala Oil

1 Cup Peanut Oil
1 Cup Sesame Oil
½ Cup	Scallion Rings (green and white. Sliced thinly)
15ea Ginger Coins Smashed
2T Szechuan Peppercorns
2T Red Chili Flakes
2t Kosher Salt


Method:
Combine all ingredients in sauce pot and slowly bring liquid up to 225 degrees.  Simmer for fifteen minutes, do not exceed 225 degrees!!!  Remove from heat and let liquid slowly cool to room temperature.  Once cool strain through chinois, DO NOT PRESS GOOP!!! 
Store in walk in.













Garnish:
2 Ea Chili Pods
1 T HK garlic
1 T Thinly Sliced Red Fresno
6 Ea Scallion Baton
¼ Cup Red Onion Julienne
6 Ea Blanched Budding Chive
10 Ea Chinese Eggplant Baton (Fried on pick up)
6 Ea Budding Chive Tops
10 Ea Yellow Chive
2 t green aromatics
¼ Cup Blanched Snow Peas
¼ Cup Blanched Snap Peas
1 t Ginger, julienned, fine
4 Cups Fried Baby Spinach (Fry at 370 degrees, remove when all water is cooked out. Remove spinach from wok and season with curry salt).  (One cup kosher salt to ½ cup S&B Curry powder.)  

Method:
Heat eight ounces of peanut oil in wok until slight smoke, add marinated beef to oil and flash fry for ten seconds.  Remove cooked beef from oil and set aside with Peas, Fried Eggplant, Yellow Chives.  Wipe wok clean and add one ounce peanut oil, smoke chilies until black. Add HK garlic and cook until it takes on light color. Add all remaining ingredients into wok, stir fry for thirty seconds.  Add two teaspoons of green aromatics in oil, one tablespoon sugar and one teaspoon wok sambal.  Cook until fragrant.  Add beef and toss to combine.  Add 4 ½ ounces of "Sweet Hot Bean Sauce" and slurry to desired thickness.  Add remaining vegetables to wok and toss together.  Put in middle of bowl with fried spinach around plate.
