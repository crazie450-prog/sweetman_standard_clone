FRENCH GREEN LENTIL SOUP 
 
Prep time: 15 minutes 
Cook time: 20 minutes 
Yield: 2 quarts 
 
 
Ingredients: 
organic French green lentils | 2 cups
yellow onion, small dice | ½ cup
celery, small dice | ½ cup
garlic, micro plane | 3 cloves
serrano pepper, small dice | 1/3 cup
chicken stock | 6 cups
bay leaf | 2 each
thyme | 10 sprigs
salt & black pepper | to taste
tomato concasse | ½ cup
 
 
 
Method: 
Blanch to lentils in boiling salted water and rinse in cold water. In a soup pot sweat the vegetables until they are for 4 minutes covered. Add the lentils and chicken stock and bring to a simmer. Make a sachet for the bay leaf and thyme and add it to the soup. When the lentils are tender season with salt and pepper. Puree ¼ of the soup in a high powered blender. Combine puree with soup and tomatoes.
