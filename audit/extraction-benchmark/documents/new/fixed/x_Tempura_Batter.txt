Tempura Batter

½ cup			rice flour
½ cup			cornstarch
1 cup			Ice water 

Method:

Combine rice flour and cornstarch, add the water.

4 cups			AP flour
2T			Baking powder
5 cups			Soda Water
1 ½ TB		Salt
2 tsp			Cayenne

Sift the flour into a bowl, and add the baking powder, salt, and cayenne pepper.
Add the rice flour mixture.
Whisk in the soda water.  You may need a bit more or less water depending on consistency desired.
