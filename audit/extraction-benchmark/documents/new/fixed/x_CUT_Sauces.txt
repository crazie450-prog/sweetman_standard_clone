Sauces:

Whole Grain Mustard:
Shallots, garlic, brandy, black pepper, Dijon mustard, grain mustard, cream.

Wasabi-Yuzu Kocho Butter:
Yuzu Kocho (salted and preserved yuzu skin & chilies), yuzu juice, shallots, garlic, parsley, buerre blanc. 

Chimichurri:
Parsley, oregano, white onion, garlic, red wine vinegar, O.O.

Bordelaise:
Shallots, garlic, black pepper, thyme, red wine & port wine reduction, butter.

Armagnac & Green Peppercorn:
Armagnac, red wine & port wine reduction, shallots, garlic, Dijon mustard, cream, green and black peppercorns.

Horseradish:
Crème Fraiche, chives, shallots, lemon juice, horseradish. 

Béarnaise: (Hollandaise with tarragon)
Champagne vinegar and shallot reduction, lemon juice, egg yolks, clarified butter, tarragon.

House Made Steak Sauce:
Horseradish, ketchup, black pepper, garlic, soy sauce, brown sugar, tamarind, bay leaf and onions.
