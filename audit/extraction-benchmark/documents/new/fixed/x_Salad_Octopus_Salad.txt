Warm Baby Octopus Salad 

2.5 Oz Cooked Baby Octopus Tentacles, cut in to halves
1 Oz Octopus Cooking Liquid
2 T Garbanzo Beans
1 T Roasted Baby Carrots
¼ tsp Garlic, Chopped
1 pinch Chili Flakes
¼ tsp Parsley, Chopped
2 Basil Leaves
3 Petals of Confit Tomato, cut into quarters
1 tsp Charmoula
1 T + 2 tsp Tuscan Evo
¼ wedge of Lemon
12 leaves Baby Arugula
Salt and Black Pepper to Taste 

Method:

Put 1 T Evo and chopped garlic in a small sauté pan over medium-high heat.  Once garlic starts to brown add chili flakes, basil, octopus and liquid, garbanzo beans, carrots and tomato.  Cook together until liquid reduces to a glaze.  Remove from heat add parsley and 1 tsp Evo.  Lay everything in the middle of a twelve inch 12" round. Top with Arrugula leaves, lemon juice, Olive Oil and Charmoula. 

Confit Tomatoes

20ea vine ripe tomatoes
2.5T sugar
3.5t salt
1t white pepper, ground
1¼C olive oil
20ea garlic cloves, smashed
25ea thyme, sprigs

Blanch tomatoes for 6 seconds in boiling water.
Shock in an ice bath
Remove the skin of the tomato, cut into quarters 
Remove the guts of the tomato so you are left with just the peddles
Line the peddles on to a sheet pan with a silk pat 
Using all of the sugar, salt, and pepper evenly season the top of the tomatoes 
Drizzle the olive oil on top of the tomatoes so each one is well coated 
Disperse the garlic and thyme on top of the tomatoes
Cook in a 300°F oven fan off 
Cook for about 45 minutes

Octopus Cooking Technique

8# Octopus, beaks and Bodies removed
½ Cup Extra  Virgin Olive Oil
2 White Onions, Peeled and Rough Chopped
2 Carrots, Peeled and Rough Chopped
3 Stalks Celery, Rough Chopped
4 Cups White Wine
4 Cups Red Wine
10 Sprigs Parsley
10 Sprigs Thyme
2 Bay Leaves
2 Sprigs Rosemary
2 Garlic Heads, Cut in half
1.5 Gallon Chicken Stock
¾ Cup Kosher Salt
¼ Cup Sugar
1 Wine Bottle Cork

In large pot over medium heat add oil, mirepoix, garlic and caramelize.  Add wine, herbs and reduce to sweet.  Add remaining ingredients. Cover with a parchment lid. Turn burner down to the lowest setting.  Cook until Octopus is tender (Aprox. 3 hours)

Charmoula

½ Cup Parsley, Finely Minced
½ Cup Cilantro, Finely Minced
¼ Cup Mint, Finely Minced
2 Cloves Garlic
1 T Cumin, toasted and finely crushed
1 tsp Coriander, toasted and finely crushed
1 tsp caraway, toasted and finely crushed
2 Zest of 2 lemons, finely minced
1 Lemon Juiced
2 tsp Saffron
1 1/3 Cup Extra Virgin Olive Oil
1 tsp Sweet Paprika
½ Cup Minced Yellow Onion
2 tsp Salt
1 tsp Pepper

Macerate Garlic cloves with the salt into a fine puree.  Combine the garlic with the rest of the ingredients in a small bowl.  Keep at room temperature.
