Rich's offers a variety of donuts in different formats and flavors:
Donut types
 Ready-to-Finish Donuts: These are pre-fried and frozen, requiring only thawing and a quick bake before finishing with glazes, icings, or other toppings.
o Examples include homestyle rings, jumbo cake donuts, twisted yeast donuts, Pershing round yeast donuts, and various donut holes.
 Fully Finished Donuts: These are ready-to-serve donuts that have been glazed, iced, or otherwise topped.
o Flavors include glazed yeast ring, chocolate iced yeast ring, glazed cinnamon roll, glazed apple fritter, and chocolate iced sprinkled cake ring.
 Donut Dough: Rich's also offers donut dough for those who prefer to fry their own donuts from scratch.

Preparation (for ready-to-finish)
1. Thaw donuts at room temperature for 30-60 minutes.
2. (Optional for glazing/sugaring) Preheat oven to 375°F and bake for 2-3 minutes.
3. Finish as desired: glaze immediately after heating, sugar while warm, or ice once cooled.
