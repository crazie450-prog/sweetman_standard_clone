CHILEAN SEABASS WTIH LEMON FENNEL CHUTNEY 
 
Prep time: 15 minutes cook time: 30 minutes 
yield: 1 cup 
 
Ingredients: 
8 individual 6 ounce 1 inch thick Chilean sea bass portions 
1 teaspoon fine ground sea salt 
¼ teaspoon granulated garlic 
½ teaspoon freshly ground white pepper 
1 bottle la tourangelle olive oil spray 
1 lemon cut to 6 wedges and seeds removed 
 
Lemon fennel chutney 
 
Method: 
Season the fish with fine ground sea salt, granulated garlic and freshly ground white pepper. Spray a hot cast iron skillet with olive oil.  
Place the fish in the cast iron skillet and let cook not moving it until it becomes golden brown and caramelized. Turn the fish over using a fish spatula. Cook the fish about 3 more minutes or until it becomes opaque throughout and it flakes easily. Remove the fish from the pan, squeeze fresh lemon juice over the fish and top with a tablespoon of lemon fennel chutney.  
 

LEMON FENNEL CHUTNEY: 
1 medium yellow onion, small dice ½  
1 medium bulb fennel, small dice 
¼ teaspoon kosher salt 
1 cup water 
½ teaspoon brown mustard seeds 
1 teaspoon honey 
1 teaspoon coriander ground 
3 lemons, zest and juice 
¼ cup golden raisins, chopped 
½ cup rice vinegar 
½ cup champagne vinegar 
¼ cup Pernod 
 ¼ teaspoon fresh ground black pepper 
16 fresh cilantro leaves, rough chopped 
 
Method: In a small, covered pot on low burner, sweat the diced onion, fennel and salt. While fennel mixture is cooking place the remaining ½ onion, water, honey, coriander, garlic, lemon juice, raisins, mustard seeds, rice vinegar, champagne vinegar in a blender and puree until smooth. When the fennel mixture is translucent, deglaze the pan with the Pernod and add the lemon juice mixture from the blender and reduce leaving off the pan lid. When the mixture is thickened remove it from the heat. Season with ground black pepper and when it is cooled to room temperature, add the chopped cilantro leaf.
