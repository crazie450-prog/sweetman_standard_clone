Fried Chicken Sandwich Recipe

Ingredients:

For the Fried Chicken:

	•	2 boneless, skinless chicken breasts (butterflied or pounded thin)
	•	1 cup buttermilk
	•	1 teaspoon hot sauce (optional, for marinade)
	•	1 cup all-purpose flour
	•	1/2 cup cornstarch
	•	1 teaspoon paprika
	•	1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
	•	1/2 teaspoon onion powder
	•	Salt and pepper to taste
	•	Vegetable oil for frying

For the Sandwich:

	•	4 brioche buns (toasted)
	•	4 slices of tomato
	•	Iceberg lettuce leaves
	•	Pickles (optional)
	•	Mayonnaise

Instructions:

	1.	Marinate the Chicken:
Place the chicken breasts in a bowl with buttermilk and hot sauce (if using). Let them marinate in the fridge for at least 1 hour, or overnight for extra tenderness.
	2.	Prepare the Coating:
In a shallow dish, mix the flour, cornstarch, paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and pepper. This flour-cornstarch combination helps create a crispy coating.
	3.	Heat the Oil:
Pour vegetable oil into a deep skillet or fryer, enough to submerge the chicken halfway. Heat the oil to about 350°F (175°C).
	4.	Coat the Chicken:
Remove the chicken from the buttermilk, allowing excess to drip off, and dredge it in the flour mixture, pressing firmly to ensure an even coating.
	5.	Fry the Chicken:
Carefully place the chicken breasts into the hot oil, frying for about 4-5 minutes per side, or until golden brown and cooked through (internal temperature should reach 165°F / 74°C). Transfer to a wire rack or paper towel-lined plate to drain any excess oil.
	6.	Assemble the Sandwich:
Spread a generous layer of mayonnaise on the bottom half of each toasted brioche bun. Place the fried chicken on top, followed by tomato slices, iceberg lettuce, and pickles (if using). Close the sandwich with the top bun.
	7.	Serve:
Serve the fried chicken sandwich immediately with a side of fries or a light salad.
