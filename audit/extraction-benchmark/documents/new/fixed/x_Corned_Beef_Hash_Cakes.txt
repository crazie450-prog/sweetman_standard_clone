CORN BEEF HASH CAKES
With Sautéed Spinach, Poached Eggs, Red Wine Reduction and Béarnaise


Ingredients:      
7 cups		Corn Beef, medium diced
5 cups		Yukon Potatoes (Brought up to a boil and cooked till fork tender, cooled 
		At room temperature, Peeled then ½ put through a large hole box grater & ½ Diced by Hand)
2 cup		Yellow Onion, medium diced
Scratch 	Nutmeg
1/8 cup		Parsley, chopped
2 cups 3 t	Butter
¼ cup 		Olive Oil 
2 bunches 	Leeks, julienne 
½ cup 		Red Wine
		Béarnaise Sauce
		Salt, Sugar, Black Pepper to taste



Procedure For Hash Cakes:
Note:    One order is two hash cakes (2.25 oz. per each hash cake)
In large stock pot, sweat onion out in butter till translucent, add corn beef and continue sweating till warm all the way through. 
Add potatoes, mix well and check for seasoning. 
Finish with parsley and lay out on a sheet pan with parchment paper, allow to cool.
In a small cast iron pan on medium heat add olive oil. 
Place hash cakes in pan; allow it to get good color and then flip.  
Add butter and place in oven at 350 degrees with fan. 


Plate-up:

Sautee spinach (already blanched ahead of time) in 2 teaspoons of butter and set aside. 
Place both hash cakes side by side on a large oval plate, place spinach on top of cakes and press down gently with your thumb and make a small well for the poached eggs to lay on. 
Add béarnaise and red wine. 
Garnish with julienne leeks that have been soaked in water and fried at 300 degrees in peanut oil.
