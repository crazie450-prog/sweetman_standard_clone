Creamy Parmesan Polenta

1 Gallon Whole Milk
½ Gallon Water
3 T Salt
6 Cups Polenta

…….

8 C Cooked Polenta
1 C Crème Fraiche
½ C Mascarpone
1 C Grated Parmesan
Block Parmesan
Tuscan Olive Oil
Fresh Ground Black Pepper

In a large heavy duty pot bring Milk water and salt to a boil.  Slowly wisk in Polenta and bring heat down to a very low temperature. Cover and cook for 30 minutes, stiring occasionally.  Pour out cooked polenta into a hotel pan and cover with plastic and cool.

Take 8 cups cooked polenta and place in medium heavy duty pot.  Add crème fraiche, marscarpone.  Bring polenta up to high temperature on a low burner stirring every five minutes.  Once polenta is very hot add parmesan cheese.

On the plate up ladle finished polenta in to a small bowl. Grind a little black pepper, drizzle with olive oil and with a microplaner grate parmesan over until a small layer forms over the polenta.
