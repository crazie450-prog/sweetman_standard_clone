Huckleberry Compote

Ingredients:
2 c. Red Wine
2 c. Port Wine
1 c. Plum Wine
½ piece Star Anise
½ piece Cinnamon Stick
2 c. Huckleberries
¾ c. Sugar

Method:
In a large sauté pan add all the wine, sugar and spices together and reduce ¾ of the way down. Once reduced and sauce is a thick consistency remove all spices and add in huckleberries, began to warm and cote berries in sauce. The berries will extract natural liquid to thin out the sauce which you will want cook for 3 minutes be careful not to overcook the berries.
