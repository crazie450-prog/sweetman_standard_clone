BONELESS BEEF SHORTRIBS, BORDEAX BRAISING JUS  
 
Prep time: 20 minutes 
Cook time:2 ½  hours  
Yield:6 portions 
 
Ingredients: 
2 ½ pounds boneless chuck short rib, fat trimmed  
1 teaspoon sea salt 
1 teaspoon canola oil 
2 medium yellow onions, medium dice 
2 medium carrots, medium dice 
2 ribs celery, medium dice  
3 cloves of garlic, peeled, smashed 
1 teaspoon tomato paste 
½ teaspoon dried ground chipotle pepper 
2 cups Bordeaux wine 
1 tablespoon worcestershire sauce 
3 sprigs of rosemary 
3 cups Bonafide provisions restorative beef bone broth 
½ teaspoon freshly ground black pepper 
 
 
Method: 
Preheat the oven to 350*F. Cut the short rib to 6 uniform 6.5 ounce portions (they shrink during cooking process). Season the beef with salt and pepper. Sear the beef in a roasting pan containing the canola oil over medium­high heat on the stove top. Turn the short ribs after 2­3 minutes or when they are rich in color. Remove the beef from the pan and set to the side. Add the onions, garlic, carrots and celery to the skillet. When the vegetables are rich in color add the tomato paste and chipotle pepper. Deglaze the skillet with red wine and worcestershire sauce.  Add the beef and rosemary, cover ⅔ of the way with the beef broth, and bring up to a simmer. Cover the beef, first with parchament paper then with aluminum foil, and transfer to the oven. Cook the ribs for 2 ½  hours or until fork­tender and pulls apart easily. Let the ribs cool in the liquid until it can be easily handled. Lift the beef out of the pan and strain the braising liquid through a chinois. 
Return the beef to a cooking vessel just large enugh to hold the cooked ribs and reserved ½ of the braising liquid. Discard the vegetables and transfer the second  ½ of the liquid to a sauce pot to reduce until it gets thick enough to coat the back of a spoon. To serve, heat the short ribs until they are hot all the way through, lift them from the braising liquid, add fresh ground black pepper to the reduced sauce and coat the beef evenly.
