Confit Bacon

Ingredients:
1 Slab Bacon
2 Sprigs Rosemary
1 head Garlic, Cut in Half
Rendered Duck Fat, To Cover

Cut bacon into blocks.
Put bacon in Hotel pan.
Add the rosemary and garlic.
Cover with duck fat.
Cover with parchment.
Cook in oven at low heat till bacon is tender.
Should take approx. 2-3 hours.
Remove to new pan.
Cover with parchment paper, add fat
Press with perforated hotel pan, tie with string.
Press for 24 hours till block is formed.
