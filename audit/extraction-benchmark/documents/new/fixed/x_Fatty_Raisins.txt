	wolfgang puck bar and grill
	recipe name: Fatty Raisins
	yield:
qty.	product
10 lb	raisins, Thompson Seedless (white Raisins)
1 gal	white wine
8 oz	orange juice
8 oz	Myers Rum
1 lb	sugar
	method:
1	In a heavy metal saucepan, add all ingredients in the order given
2	place over a medium flame, stir
3	cook for 10 minutes, stir, raisins should just come to a boil
4	lower the flame and continue to poach for 20 minutes
5	remove from heat, cover with plastic wrap
6	cool to room temperature
