CHICKEN & ANDOUILLE GUMBO


1#		Flour
1#		Butter
2Cup		Garlic
3 Cups		Onions
2 Cups		Bell Peppers
2 Cups		Celery
1 @ 4 2 #	Chicken (Roaster Cooked in Stock and Then Picked)
1 gal		Chicken Stock (from the Roaster)
2 #		Andouille Sausage
¾ Cup		Tomato Paste 
Creole Spices (Blackening Spices)
Bay Leaves
Thyme (dried) 
File Powder
Hot Sauce (Frank=s or Crystal)
Salt & Pepper
Sugar

1 cup		Green Onions (green & whites)
Parsley Chopped (garnish)

Make Roux with Flour& Butter (Nice and Brown) cook it slowly in oven until chestnut color. 
In large pot sweat garlic, Vegetables, Then Add Spices, S& P, Herbs, File and Paste
Add Roux to Pot and Combine Well 
Add in Chicken Stock, and Bring to Boil. Slow to a Simmer until Flour Is Cooked
Then Add Chicken Meat, Sausage and Hot Sauce, Adjust Seasonings, Simmer 
Serve over Rice and Top with Parsley and Green Onions
