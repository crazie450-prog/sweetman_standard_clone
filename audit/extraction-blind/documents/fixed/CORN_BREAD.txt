Corn Bread



4# 			Butter
48			 Eggs
1 ½ C 			Honey (micro)
9#8oz 			AP Flour
4# 			Sugar
1# 			 P. Milk
3oz 			Salt
2# 8oz 			Corn Meal
9oz 			Baking Powder
1G			 Milk
2C 			Jalapenos (chopped)

Cream butter, until light and fluffy
sift Dry
Slowly add 1/8 eggs and honey
then dry
Repeat
Add Milk
Jalapenos
