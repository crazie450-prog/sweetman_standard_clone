Crepes
Makes 24 6" crepes

SIFT:

2/3	cups 		All purpose flour
1/4  	cups		Granulated sugar
2 	teaspoons	Ground ginger
1/4 	teaspoons 	Salt

WHISK TOGETHER:

1 cup + 2 Tbl.	Milk
4			Eggs
5 	Tbl.		Clarified butter

PROCEDURE:

Add sifted dry ingredients to liquid - whisk until smooth. Let batter rest for 30 minutes. Brush pan with clarified butter.
