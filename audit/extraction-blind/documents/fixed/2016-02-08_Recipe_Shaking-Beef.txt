Shaking Beef

FOR THE MARINADE
2 pounds boneless beef sirloin, fillet, or rib eye, cut
10 garlic cloves, minced
1 shallot
1 tablespoon Maggi seasoning or soy sauce
2 tablespoon sugar
1/4 cup fish sauce 
1 teaspoon sea salt
2 tablespoons black pepper

Blend. And marinade for at least 2 hours 

Dredge with cornstarch and fry...
