Greek Hummus

#10 Chick Peas "Garbanzos"(Canned, Drained)
3 cup Tahini
1 ½ c Lemon Juice
½ c Garlic (Chopped Fine)
¼ c Cumin Seed (Ground)
To taste salt



Procedure:

Grind Chick Peas and Hold Aside.
In a Blender Process Remaining Ingredients.
In Processor Combine The Two until Nice and Thick But Seasoned Well
Thin With remaining Bean Juices or Water

Garnish:

Olive oil
