ARF Mustard Vinaigrette
Yields 1 Gallon
3 cu Grain Mustard
2 cu Dijon Mustard
6 cu White Balsamic Vinegar
8 cu Blended Olive Oil
8 Tbsp Honey
8 Tbsp Sugar
8 Tbsp Chopped Chives
Salt and pepper to season
1. Combine all ingredients except the blended olive oil
2. Whisk in the blended olive oil to emulsify the dressing
3. Season with salt and pepper
4. Label and date
