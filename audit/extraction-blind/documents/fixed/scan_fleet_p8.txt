Fleetwood's on Front St
Coconut Curry Cream
Yield: 6 Qts
3quarts Cream Friche
1cup Dijon Mustard
4Tbsp Green Curry Paste
4Tbsp Curry Powder
10cans Coconut Milk
6inches Ginger
4stalks Lemon Grass (smashed)
1bunch Cilantro
1each Salt and Pepper (to season)
1. Place all ingredients in a pot.
2. Bring to a boil.
3. Let it steep until cool.
4. Strain.
5. Label and date
