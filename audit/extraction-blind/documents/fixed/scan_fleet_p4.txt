Fleetwood's on Front St
Mashed Potatoes
15each Baking Potatoes (peeled)
2lbs Cubed Butter (room temperature)
2cups Hot Cream
1each Salt (to season)
1-1/2x Water
1. Cut potatoes in 1/2 and add to a pot
2. Season with salt and add 1 1/2 times water.
3. Boil until tender and strain, leave the potatoes in the strainer for 5 minutes to dry
4. Place the cubed butter in the bottom of a baine marie and rice the potatoes of the butter to melt
5. Mix with a rubber scraper, add hot cream to consistency and re-season with salt
6. Transfer to a service container and cover with plastic
7. Label and date
Keep cream on the station in order to reheat potatoes
