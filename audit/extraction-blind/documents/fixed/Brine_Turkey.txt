Turkey Brine

3 gal. Water
3 c. red vinegar  
1 ½ C Coarse Sea Salt
1 ½ C Kosher Salt
8 med. white onions
½ Bunch Celery
2 Med Carrots
1 C. garlic cloves (smashed)
½ C.  Black peppercorns
10 bay leaves
1/8 c. juniper
1 small Bunch thyme
1 Small Bunch Rosemary
1 Bunch Parsley

1 cup mustard seed
1/8 c. cloves
1/4 c. allspice
2 c. coriander

Rough Cut All Vegetables. Heat together everything but  Herbs. Bring to boil. Simmer 10 Minutes add Herbs, Remove from stove.

Brine Turkey for 36-48 hrs.
